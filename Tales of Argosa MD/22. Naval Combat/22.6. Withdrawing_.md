At any time prior to boarding, either ship may attempt to withdraw. The Captains make **opposed Int (Watercraft) checks** (PCs roll for their ship). Any modifiers are GM's call. If the PCs' ship is withdrawing, they may also attempt a Group *Luck* save to escape. If the enemy ship is seeking to withdraw, the party may attempt a Group *Luck* save to force either Captain to reroll. The winner decides whether the fleeing vessel escapes.
