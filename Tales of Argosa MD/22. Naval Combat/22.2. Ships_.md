**Ship traits** are simplified into **crew** (min - max), **speed**, and **arms.** The below statistics are defaults only and specific vessels may differ as the GM determines. As outlined later, **relative differences** between ship statistics may **may result in modifiers** to Artillery, Ramming, Boarding, or Withdrawing tests, as adjudicated by the referee.
