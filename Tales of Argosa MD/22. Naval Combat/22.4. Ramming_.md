After Artillery fire has been resolved, either ship may **choose to ram** the other. Both Captains make an **opposed Int (Watercraft) check** (PCs roll for their ship). Any modifiers are GM's call. PCs may attempt a Group *Luck* save to allow their Captain to reroll. The winner makes a **1d20 Artillery roll** to determine the damage caused (reroll misses), and the vessels are locked together until boarding is resolved. Either crew may **immediately board**.
