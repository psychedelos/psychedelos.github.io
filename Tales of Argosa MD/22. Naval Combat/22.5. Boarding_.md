After Artillery fire and ramming (if any) have been resolved, either ship may **choose to board** the other, using grapple lines, etc. Ship melee is a **chaotic mess** of sudden assaults and shrapnel blasts, with crew dropping from rigging, sliding across decks, and negotiating various levels, holds, etc.

To reflect his, **one PC** rolls on the **[[22.5.1. Boarding|Boarding]] table** at the **start** of their turn each round: **1d20** if **above deck** or **1d10** if **below deck**. Rotate which PC rolls each round. The **GM decides** who the PCs must face during the boarding (or default to **Captain along with 2d6 crew**).

### Crew vs Crew

At the end of each PC round, resolve crew fighting with a **2d6 roll**, applying a 1 or 2 point modifier for clearly superior forces. Usual **Morale** rules apply.
