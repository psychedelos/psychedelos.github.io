As the two ships approach each other, each makes **two Artillery rolls** against their foe (reflecting a series of barrages). If facing more than one hostile vessel, the attacker may divide their rolls. Each attack is resolved **simultaneously** with the enemy's unless the GM decides otherwise.

**Roll 1d20 for each Artillery strike.** Any **modifiers** are at the **GM's discretion**. For example, a war ship fighting a sloop might roll 1d20+3 (higher results are more serious). If a result is **inapplicable**, treat as a miss or a cosmetic hit. Unless the GM decides otherwise, **crew** cannot be reduced below their minimum number via Artillery, and **broken** crew take no further meaningful part in the battle unless the party rallies them.

**Heavy Cannons** cost 7,000 sp, **Light Cannons** are 4,000 sp, **Onagers** 500 sp, and **Ballistae** 400 sp.

### Repairs

The GM decides whether ship repairs can be made at sea or if the vessel must be dry docked. Rigging, mast, rudder, etc **repairs** are normally **10%** of ship price, or full **replacements 30%**. For **Artillery** weapon repairs see [[9.12. Repairs & Crafting_|p.61]].
