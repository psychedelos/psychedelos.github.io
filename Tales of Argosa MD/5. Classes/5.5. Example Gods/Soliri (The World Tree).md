Soliri is the goddess of the sun, weather, nature, and creation. She is implored to bless harvests, encourage fertility, and to repel darkness or ferocious beasts. Commonly depicted as a female faced sun, a swarm of leaves, or a giant world tree.

**Related Activities** Planting, harvesting, breeding, weather ceremonies, hunting, camping, surveying, raising bonfires. Her feast day is Long Harvest.

**Common Phrases** *The World Tree provides. Man too is a force of nature. Burn back the shadow!*

**Icons** Flaring sun. Stylized leaf. Colossal tree with great roots.

**Tenets**

- *Venerate the sun and flame.*
- *Explore the uncharted wilds.*
- *Give to the earth, take from the earth.*
- *Reject the hollow adornments and luxuries of convention.*
- *Root out and destroy abomination in all its forms.*

**Patron Benefits**

- You may roll against Will on checks relating to Wilderness Lore.
- Add the following to Spell like Blessings: *[[Bestial Communion|Bestial Communion]], [[One with the Deep|One with the Deep]], [[Thunderous Invocation|Thunderous Invocation]].*
