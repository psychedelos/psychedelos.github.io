Magic is not only rare, it is dark and inherently dangerous. Sorcery was not meant for mortals, and this fundamental mismatch taints every casting with uncertainty. From beyond the Veil, magic draws the attention of strange and inscrutable forces, some of which find ways to impose their will on the material realm.

Every time a character **attempts** to: (i) cast a **spell**, (ii) invoke a **blessing without favour**, (iii) use a **magical item**, or (iv) activate some other magical effect as the GM determines, a check must be made to determine whether a *Dark & Dangerous Magic* effect (or possibly *[[15.6.1. Divine Rebuke|Divine Rebuke]]*) occurs. Magical items that grant **permanent "always on"** bonuses or effects (eg immunity to fire, or a crit range boost) are an exception and **exempt** from DDM checks.

When making a DDM roll, the **type of die** used depends on the character's level or whether they are using a magical item:

### DDM Die

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1-3** | 1d8 | **Level 7-9** | 1d12 |
| **Level 4-6** | 1d10 | **Magic Item** | 1d20 |

If the roll is a **natural 1**, the magic functions as usual, but roll **1d100** for an **additional effect** from the DDM table (or, if a blessing, from the *[[15.6.1. Divine Rebuke|Divine Rebuke]]* table). If a DDM or DR effect **does not** occur, the chance **increases by 1** for each further attempt to use magic until an effect triggers or the adventure ends. A PC's DDM tally **resets to 1** when an effect triggers or a new adventure begins.

> [!example]
> *Old Man Wu* is a 3rd level Magic User with a DDM tally of 5. He attempts to cast another spell and succeeds. As a 3rd level character, he then rolls 1d8 to check for DDM. He rolls a 4, triggering a DDM effect! After the effect is resolved, *Old Man Wu's* DDM tally resets to 1.

Unless otherwise noted, DDM and *[[15.6.1. Divine Rebuke|Divine Rebuke]]* effects are self sustaining and last a minimum of one minute; they cannot be ended early by mortal magic. At the GM's option, particularly deleterious effects might be lessened or reversed with a custom [[15.10. Ritual Magic_|ritual]].
