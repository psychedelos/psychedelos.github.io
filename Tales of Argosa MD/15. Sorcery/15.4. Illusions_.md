Some magical effects create illusions fooling vision, hearing, smell, taste, and sense of temperature. No illusion can mimic the sense of tactile sensation however, and interacting with one will reveal the ruse, for example by passing a hand or object through it. At any time, a character may attempt to disbelieve a suspected illusion, which may require an **Int check** at the GM's discretion. If successful, the character observes the figment as a transparent overlay covering the real creature, object, or terrain. Illusions cannot directly damage or physically affect a target.
