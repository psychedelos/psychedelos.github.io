Any character with the **Arcane Lore** skill may spend a few minutes attempting to sense magic in a Close area by making an **Int (Arcane Lore\*)** check. If successful the character senses there is magic either present or absent, perhaps as a tingling through their spine, a barely perceivable hum in the air, or some other fleeting quality that vanishes after only a moment. **Illusion magic** cannot be detected in this way, part of its power includes fooling rudimentary detection methods of this kind.
