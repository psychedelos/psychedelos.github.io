**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** Special

You attempt to dispel an existing magical effect. Make an opposed Int (Arcane Lore) check against the other caster (or Int 1d4+12, if the original caster is unknown). On a success a temporary effect is ended, or a permanent enchantment suspended for 2d6 minutes. On a fail, roll on the DDM table instead, and you cannot repeat the attempt until you gain a level. On a Great Success the owner of the targeted effect does not realise their spell has been broken (otherwise they generally do sense such; GM discretion applies).
