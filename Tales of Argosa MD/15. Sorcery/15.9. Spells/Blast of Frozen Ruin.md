**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** Instant

Freezing hoarfrost and ice blasts outward from you to fill a cone shaped area (up to Close sized at the widest point; GM decides how many creatures are affected). Targets suffer 1d6 damage per caster level, on a Great Success roll damage with adv. A *Luck* (Dex) save halves the damage, on a failed save the target is also quasi frozen, unable to move from their location (treat as *[[12.11. Conditions_#Grabbed|Grabbed]]* vs Str 13).
