**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 minutes

You conjure an enchanted darkness that fills a Close sized area, impenetrable to all non magical vision (*[[12.11. Conditions_#Blind|Blind]]*). Bringing a magical light into the area softens the pitch blackness to semi opaque, reducing the penalty to *[[12.11. Conditions_#Near Blind|Near Blind]]*. On a Great Success, the darkness is unaffected by magical light.
