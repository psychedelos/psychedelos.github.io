**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 minutes

This enchantment causes 4d4 [[21.1. Monsters_#Hit Dice|HD]] worth of living humanoids in a Close sized area to fall asleep (*[[12.11. Conditions_#Helpless|Helpless]]*). Lower HD humanoids are targeted first, and a *Luck* (Will) save resists. Targets of 5 HD or more are immune. Shaking a victim grants a Will check to awaken. On a Great Success, you may target other living creatures.
