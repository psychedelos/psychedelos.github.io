**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** 2d6 hours

One object produces light, from a dim glow to a bright radiance that illuminates a Far sized area (action to change brightness). Alternatively you may cast this spell on a target's eyes to *[[12.11. Conditions_#Blind|Blind]]* it (*Luck* (Con) save negates). On a Great Success, you may produce sunlight, and if the object strikes a *[[Demons|Demon]]* or *Undead*, they suffer 1d6 extra damage.
