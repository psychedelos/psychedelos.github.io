**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

You animate an object (max 1 HD per caster level, use *[[Animated Object|Animated Object]]* stats as a guide) which gains a degree of flexibility, enabling it to fight, walk, slither, etc (GM decides Move rate). The object follows your mental commands as part of your action. On a Great Success, you may animate two objects (total HD must be your level or less). When this spell ends, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]] (unlikely)* to determine whether the animating spirit breaks free and manifests uncontrolled for 1d4 minutes (roll 1d3: (i) *[[Shade|Shade]]*, (ii) *[[Ghost|Ghost]]*, (iii) *[[Wraith|Wraith]]*).
