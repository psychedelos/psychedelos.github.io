**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** Special

A barrage of stones hammers a Close sized area, causing 1d6 damage per caster level, on a Great Success roll damage with advantage. A *Luck* (Con) save halves the damage, on a Terrible Failure also *[[13.2.2. Blast Trauma|Blast Trauma]]* (1d6). The area is slippery with scree for three rounds; Dex check or *[[12.11. Conditions_#Prone|Prone]]* if attempting to move more than half move speed.
