**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 mins

You conjure a complex illusion, causing the target to look like (but not identical to) another humanoid you have seen, including their clothes, armour, and gear. The target's real body and gear must be disguised as something else, nothing is turned invisible. The illusion is convincing but not perfect; if attempting to mimic a specific uniform or item, close inspection by someone familiar with the real article allows a Perc/Int check to spot the deception (on a Great Success, attempts to see through the illusion require a Perc/Int Great Success).
