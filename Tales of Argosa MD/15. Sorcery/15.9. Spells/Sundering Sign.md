**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** Instant

The *Sundering Sign* opens, unlocks, and unbars all locks, doors, gates, windows, manacles, bear traps, and similar objects within range, including those held or locked by magic. Invoking the *Sign* weakens the Veil; *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]] (unlikely)* to determine whether a DDM effect occurs. On a Great Success, you may choose which items open and which remain closed.
