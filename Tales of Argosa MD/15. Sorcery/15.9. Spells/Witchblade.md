**Range:** Special  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

You conjure a spectral melee weapon (no larger than a polearm) in your hand, or enchant an existing non magical weapon that you touch. You may give the weapon to another person, and anyone wielding it counts as proficient. The weapon works as normal with a random bonus effect for the duration (roll 1d6). On a Great Success you may choose the effect.
