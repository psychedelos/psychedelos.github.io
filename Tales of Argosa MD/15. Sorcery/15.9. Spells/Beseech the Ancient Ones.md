**Range:** Self  
**Duration:** 2d6 minutes

You contact a supernatural entity mankind was not meant to know, and may force a yes or no answer to questions posed. You may ask up to five questions, which the entity answers truthfully, but perhaps cryptically or in riddles (GM's call). Afterwards, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult to Bones]] (likely)* to determine whether you suffer a random Madness. On a Great Success *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]] (unlikely)* instead.
