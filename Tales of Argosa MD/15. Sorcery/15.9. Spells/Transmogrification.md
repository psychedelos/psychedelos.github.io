**Range:** Self  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 mins

You turn into another type of creature you have touched before. The form cannot be smaller than a large rat or have more HD than half your level, rounded up (on a Great Success, half level rounded up, +2). Gain the physical qualities (eg AC, Str/Dex/Con/Perc, attack bonus, damage, movement, senses), but not magical traits or hp of the form. You retain your personality and may use spells if the form can speak and has hands. Gear melds into you unless you decide otherwise. Polymorph magic is taxing, draining 1d4 Con, which cannot be restored by any means until your next Downtime.
