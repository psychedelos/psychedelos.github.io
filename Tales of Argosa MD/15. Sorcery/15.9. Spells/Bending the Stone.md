**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** Shaping up to 2d6 minutes, but the effects are permanent.

You permanently shape stone up to the volume of a wagon into another form. The transformation process might take a few seconds to a few minutes, depending on the degree of detail involved. A rock might be shaped into a sword, box, door, passage or small room. Rudimentary moving parts may be constructed, such as hinges and a latch. On a Great Success you might be able to trap one creature: GM's call depending on the circumstances, if so a *Luck* (Dex) save resists.
