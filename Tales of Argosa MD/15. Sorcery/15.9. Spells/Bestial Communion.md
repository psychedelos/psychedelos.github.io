**Range:** Self  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 mins

This charm causes normal animals and insects (not *Giant* or *Dire*) within Close range to regard you as friendly, unless your actions suggest otherwise. You may engage in two way conversations with any animal and/or insect within range, during which time you make chittering, squawking, or similar noises. The spell imbues your communications with persuasive influence, granting advantage on any Cha checks with animals and insects. On a Great Success, you may affect up to 2d6 *Giant* or *Dire* animals or insects instead.
