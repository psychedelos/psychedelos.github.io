**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

The target of this spell gains one bonus attack when they use their action to attack. On a Great Success, you may affect a second target. When the spell ends, the target must make a Will check or is compelled to drink from the skull of a dead enemy (if prevented from doing so, they go into a murderous rage). If the Will check is a Terrible Failure the target also suffers a random Madness.
