**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

You conjure an invisible hand of force. The hand has Str, Dex, and Perc 7 (for throwing purposes), and may perform any relatively simple activity (the control is not fine enough to pick a mechanical lock, for example). Directing the hand requires your action. If the hand attacks with a weapon, apply your attack bonus (with Str or Perc 7). On a Great Success, the hand has attributes of 14.
