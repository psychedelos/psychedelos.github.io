**Range:** Self  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 minutes

You perceive the visual, aural, and olfactory auras of magic and spirit forms (including Invisible targets) within Far range. You also discern the kind of magic involved (eg protection, healing, etc) and identify any physical Veil echoes such as temperature drops, spectral winds, clinging vapours, etc. Illusions are not ordinarily detected, part of their magic includes fooling detection spells of this kind (on a Great Success, you also identify and see through illusions). *Pierce the Veil* is blocked by a thin sheet of lead, or stone, earth, or wood more than 1 ft thick.
