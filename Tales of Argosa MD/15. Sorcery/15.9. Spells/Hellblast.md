**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** Instant

You project a ball of fire that explodes in a roaring inferno, filling a Close sized area. If the fireball strikes something before reaching the intended destination, it detonates early. The blast causes 1d6 damage per caster level (*Luck* (Dex) save for half, on a Terrible Failure also *[[12.11. Conditions_#Burning|Burning]]*). On a Great Success, roll damage with advantage. *Hellblast* is very loud and ignites the surrounding environment.
