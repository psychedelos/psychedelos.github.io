**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** 2d6 mins

You conjure a tangible but invisible tennis ball sized eye through which you can see. You direct the eye by spending an action; it flies at your normal speed and may move up to Far range from you. You may switch between senses by spending an action. On a Great Success, the eye comes with an ear which you may hear through. When using the magical senses, your real eyes and/or ears do not function.
