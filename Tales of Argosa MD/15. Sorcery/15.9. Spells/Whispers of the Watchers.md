**Range:** Very Far  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 mins

You sense which direction you must travel in (as the crow flies) to reach an object or person within range. You must sufficiently describe the target or have a mental image of them. If you have not seen the target, identifying features such as a female teenager, blonde hair, with cheekbone scar, might be enough to allow the spell to work (GM's call).

The spell may also be used to detect a general type of object such as stairs, gold coins, keys, etc. The magic is blocked by a thin sheet of lead, or stone, earth, or wood more than 1 ft thick.

On a Great Success, the spell's range is increased (referee's call) or you also sense the largest concentration of objects sought. Treating with the Watchers beyond the Veil is dangerous work; when the spell ends a Will check is required to avoid suffering a random Madness.
