**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

One small metal object (exc worn clothes/armour) such as a sword or door knob turns red hot (no *Luck* save). Anyone touching the item suffers 1d8 dmg, *[[12.11. Conditions_#Burning|Burning]]* (*Luck* (Con) save negates condition only), and automatically drops or releases it. The object sheds dim light and sets fire to flammable objects. On a Great Success, you may target two objects.
