**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** 2d6 minutes

The target is immune to one kind of damaging energy or other dangerous element, commonly fire, lightning, or cold, but also for example steam, acid, or other natural hazards. On a Great Success you may choose a more unusual supernatural hazard such as a *[[Shade|Shade's]]* Str drain, a *[[Ghost|Ghost's]]* aging attack, or a *[[Spectre|Spectre's]]* level drain.
