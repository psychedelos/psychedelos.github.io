You are skilled in the lost art of enchanting items through precise, painstaking rune etchings. Learn three random spells (roll 1d100 on the Spell List). You may perform a ritual to store one of your known spells in any ring sized or larger object. Treat this process as inscribing a [[16.11. Scroll Inscribing_|Scroll]], but no spellbook or monster parts (special materials) are required. Anyone may automatically release the spell with a command word (action), applying success or Great Success to the spell effect per the creator's original Int (Arcane Lore\*) check. Each stored spell increases your min DDM tally by 1.
