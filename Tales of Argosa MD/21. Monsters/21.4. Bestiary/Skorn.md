Also known as halfmen or beastmen, *Skorn* are heavy set, pink skinned proto-humans, often scarred and heavily sunburnt. Their precise traits vary by tribe but most would be 7 ft tall but for their stooping stature, with small eyes, flat faces, rudimentary ear holes, and oversized jaws. *Skorn* are dull witted cannibals, more beast than man, preferring to eat humans than animals or their own kind. They have rudimentary dark vision, seeing rough shapes in complete darkness (*[[12.11. Conditions_#Near Blind|Near Blind]]*).
