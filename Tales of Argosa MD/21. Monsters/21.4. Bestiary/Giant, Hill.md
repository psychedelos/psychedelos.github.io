*Hill Giants* are approximately 12 ft tall, the lowest caste of the true giants. Most are primitive cave dwellers or nomads with rudimentary language, carrying clubs and crude tools.

**HILL GIANT** #1d12 **AC** 12 **HD** 8+2 **Club** 2d8+2 **Nat 19-20** *[[13.2.3. Blunt Trauma|Blunt Trauma]]* (1d6+6) **S**20 **D**8 **C**15 **I**6 **P**9 **W**12 **Ch**8 **L**9. Hurl boulder, carts, etc up to Very Far range for 3d6 damage. Olfactory sense like a dog.
