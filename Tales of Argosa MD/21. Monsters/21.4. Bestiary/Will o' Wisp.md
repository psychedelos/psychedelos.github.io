*Will o' Wisps* are malicious, translucent spheres of eerie light that hunt in remote wilderness, luring travellers to their deaths. They are *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Demons & Undead|Undead]]*, some say the vestiges of souls who died frightful deaths alone in the wilds. *Wisps* may brighten or dim their luminescence, and change shape to appear as groups of lights, shimmering pools, or glowing creatures. They commonly inhabit swamps, high moors, and dark forests. Elder *Wisps* may manifest illusions (*[[Shadows & Dust|Shadows & Dust]]* (GS), Very Far range).
