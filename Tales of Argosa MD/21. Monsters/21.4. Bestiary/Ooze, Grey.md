*Grey Ooze*, also known as grey matter ooze, is more liquid than ooze, a fast flowing and terrifying amoeba that feeds on the thoughts of living creatures. It attacks by surging into the target's mouth, nose, and ears, tunneling its way to the victim's brain which it absorbs.

*Grey Ooze* have rudimentary intelligence and possess limited telepathic abilities. They can mirror and project basic emotions such love, fear, and wonder, which they may project out to Very Far range, luring or herding their prey.

If killed, the ooze takes up residence in the host's skull, controlling them in an appalling, puppet like fashion. When it desires another meal, the ooze pours out of the host's orifices to attack.
