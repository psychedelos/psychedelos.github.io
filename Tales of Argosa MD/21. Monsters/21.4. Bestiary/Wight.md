*Wights* are semi decayed humanoid *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Demons & Undead|Undead]],* often bound to graveyards or other resting places of the departed. All *Wights* develop cursed insights into the Veil, gaining the ability to cast spells whilst falling deeper into madness. A creature killed by their dread touch rises as a *[[Zombies|Zombie]]*.
