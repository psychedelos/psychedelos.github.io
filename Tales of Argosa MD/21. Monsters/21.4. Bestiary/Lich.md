*Liches* are *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Demons & Undead|Undead]]* wizards, transformed by dark rites or dangerous magics gone wrong. Whatever the cause, the transformation inevitably turns the magic user mad; the longer the *Lich* persists, the greater its derangement. They often build up a collection of enchanted objects over their long years of magical research.
