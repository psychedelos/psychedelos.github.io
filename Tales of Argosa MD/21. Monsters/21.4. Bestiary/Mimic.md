The *Mimic* is a sorcerous oddity of shapeshifting sentience; a living trap brought to life by arcane design, catastrophe, or curse. They may change forms to impersonate an object from as small as a shoe to as large as a wagon, and are generally indistinguishable from a mundane object unless touched or threatened with fire. The *Mimic* may exude a super adhesive secretion when desired, causing weapons and creatures to stick to it. Strong alcohol acts as a solvent to free the captured.
