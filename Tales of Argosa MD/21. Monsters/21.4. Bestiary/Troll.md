*Trolls* are 10 ft tall, rubbery fleshed humanoids with large ears and hard nodules patterning their skin. They have oversized heads and teeth, paired with elongated arms and claws. *Trolls* are infamous for their power to self regenerate, making them extraordinarily difficult to kill without fire or acid on hand. In swamps or moors they sink beneath the waters to hibernate in times of scarcity, waiting for prey to rouse them.
