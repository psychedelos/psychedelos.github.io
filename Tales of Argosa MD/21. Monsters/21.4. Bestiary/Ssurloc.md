*Ssurlocs* are 8 ft tall hybrids, half humanoid, half serpent, devoid of empathy and utterly loyal to the serpentmen cause. Their venom causes a potent paralytic effect. If using a weapon, *Ssurlocs* coat their weapons with their poison.
