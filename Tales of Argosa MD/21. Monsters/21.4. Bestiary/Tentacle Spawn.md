*Tentacle Spawn* are oily skinned, psionic humanoids with octopus like heads from another planet or dimension. Their motives are inscrutable but they are intelligent and exist on a steady diet of brains. They are subterranean in nature and become severely disorientated if exposed to wide open spaces. *Tentacle Spawn* attack with psychic powers or by burrowing their tendrils into an opponent's skull to pluck out their brain.
