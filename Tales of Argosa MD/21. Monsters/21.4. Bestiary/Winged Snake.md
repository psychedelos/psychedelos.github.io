*Winged Snakes* are typically 6 ft long, dark green or brown, with hard scales and leathery bat like wings that propel them through the air in a lurching motion (somewhat akin to a bat). Bad tempered and ferocious, *Winged Snakes* are quick to strike but also quick to retreat if battle fares poorly, relying on their wings to flee. They have a venomous bite and can also spit poison a short distance.
