*Aberrant Terrors* are unholy horrors spawned from other dimensions, or magic gone catastrophically awry. *Terrors* appear in all manner of forms, and most have a special trait according to the **1d12** table below. Whatever shape the monstrosity takes, their move rate remains humanoid like, whether by means of crawling, slithering, floating, etc. All *Terrors* **regenerate** wounds, excluding those caused by silver or fire.
