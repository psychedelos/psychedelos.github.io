*Green Slime* is an immobile, predatory amoeba. It detects body heat and attacks by dropping onto moving targets from above, or flinging globs of goop. Any metal or organic substance *Green Slime* touches transforms into more *Green Slime.* If a limb or object becomes slime, it attaches to the host and spreads each round, transforming more of the victim. For example, *Green Slime* might first transform a hand, then an arm, then the torso which would kill a human. *Green Slime* is destroyed by sunlight, fire, extreme cold, or *[[Cleansing Charm|Cleansing Charm]],* which renders it an inert tar.
