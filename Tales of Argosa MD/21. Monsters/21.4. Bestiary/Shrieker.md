*Shriekers* are immobile, 7 ft mushroom fungi with sphincter like organs on top. If they sense warm bodied creatures within Far range, they emit a piercing cry for several seconds, alerting other monsters that something edible is close. Scratching the fungus on the underside of its cap in just the right way (might require a check) causes it to murmur contently then "sleep" for 1d4 hours. Some fungi have a means of defence (roll 1d6).
