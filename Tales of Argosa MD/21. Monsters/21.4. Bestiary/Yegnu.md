*Yegnu* are interdimensional aliens composed of slough like flesh that moulds to suit their purpose. In their native form they adopt a 3 ft long slug like shape, with a single eye and prehensile tendrils. They are intelligent parasites that paralyse their victim before entering through their mouth, taking up residence in the digestive system. Once ensconced, *Yegnu* control their victim via chemical signals until the host dies of starvation, at which time they exit the corpse to find another puppet. *Yegnu* avoid salt as its touch burns them.
