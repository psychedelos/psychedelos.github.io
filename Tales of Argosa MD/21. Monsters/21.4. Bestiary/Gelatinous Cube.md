*Gelatinous Cubes* are highly translucent, jelly like monsters that expand to fit the width of corridors or small chambers. They sense movement within Very Far range and are voracious predators, paralysing and absorbing all forms of organic matter. They are sometimes employed by lazy dungeon lords as inexpensive cleaners.
