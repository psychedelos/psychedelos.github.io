*Sprites* (also known as *Pixies* or *Brownies*) are 6 inch fey humanoids found in forests, woodlands, or near water. Some have gossamer wings while others have gills and are amphibious.

Sprites are notoriously capricious with a powerful lust for a small glittering things such as coins, rings, and gems. They employ a sleep inducing poison on their tiny arrows to waylay travellers, liberating them of their shiniest trinkets.
