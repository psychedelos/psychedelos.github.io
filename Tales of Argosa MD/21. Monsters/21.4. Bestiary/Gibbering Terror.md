*Gibbering Terrors* are undulating, babbling oozes of teeth, tentacles, and eyes, threatening the sanity of any who gaze upon them. They employ a kind of burbling motion, able to move up walls and across ceilings as the ground. The mind of a *Gibbering Terror,* such as it is, is wholly unintelligible; they simply seek to destroy all living creatures they come across. They make no distinction between a person, animal, insect, or fungus.
