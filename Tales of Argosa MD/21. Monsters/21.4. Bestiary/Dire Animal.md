*Dire* creatures are beasts corrupted by rifts in the Veil or cursed by demons or other malevolent forces. To apply the *Dire* template to an animal:

- Increase **AC** and **HD** by 2.
- Increase **damage** by two dice categories (eg d4 becomes d8, max d12).
- **Reac** is typically 2-7 Hostile, 8-10 Aggressive, 11-12 Cautious.
- Create a **special ability** to delight, perplex and perhaps terrorize your PCs, or roll on the *[[Urgot|Urgot]]* mutation table ([[Mutations|p.202]]).
