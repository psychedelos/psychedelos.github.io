*Bloodroot Treants* are sentient, 20 ft tall animated trees corrupted by great evil. Unlike the peaceable tree whisperers, these hardwoods hunger for flesh and blood rather than earth and stream. *Bloodroots* look like any normal tree until they attack, battering their foes with powerful limbs before digging them down into the earth and rooting themselves atop the still warm corpse. *Bloodroots* are unintelligent and incapable of speech, but do use leaf rustling to communicate very basic concepts to each other. They are particularly susceptible to fire and will retreat from forest fires and other very large blazes.
