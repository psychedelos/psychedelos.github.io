The *Doppelganger* is a predatory Veil entity able to magically assume the physical form (including clothing and gear) of any humanoid they have touched.

Complementing their physical disguise, they may subtlety skim the surface thoughts of any intelligent being within Close range, assisting them to answer queries the questioner already knows the answer to.
