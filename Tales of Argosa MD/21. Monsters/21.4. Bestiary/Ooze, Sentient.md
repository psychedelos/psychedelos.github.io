*Sentient Oozes* come in a various forms, but are invariably carnivorous flesh melters. Their amorphous mass may flatten to crawl under doors or puff up to the size of a small wagon. Most emerge from subterranean pools, fissures, or Veil ruptures. *Oozes* track their prey by various methods including scent, body heat, and vibration. They fall into stasis like hibernation if starved, roused to wakefulness if a warm body comes within Close range. Roll **1d10** to determine a particular ooze's special trait.
