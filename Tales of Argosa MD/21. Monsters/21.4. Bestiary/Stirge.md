*Stirges* resemble grotesque, 1 ft mosquitoes with six hook like appendages and a needle proboscis, used to drain their victims of blood. They swarm a target in large numbers and are difficult to remove once attached. Not unlike bees, *Stirges* may be pacified with smoke.

**STIRGE** #3d10 **AC** 12 **HD** 1d4 hp **Bite** 1d3 + Special **Nat 19** *Luck* (Con) save or disease **S**4 **D**16 **C**6 **I**2 **P**10 **W**5 **Ch**4 **L**3, **Fly** Norm. Bite victims suffer 1d4 blood drain at the end of their turn unless Str contest (S13) to pry off (action, kills the *Stirge*). Attacking a *Stirge* attached to an ally suffers a -2 penalty and on a 1-3 attack roll strikes the PC. Dousing with smoke makes them sleepy and aimless (Will check resists). If left unchecked *Stirges* will kill an unconscious PC in 1d6 rounds. **Reac** 2-7 Ravenous 8-10 Follow at a distance 11-12 Fly away.
