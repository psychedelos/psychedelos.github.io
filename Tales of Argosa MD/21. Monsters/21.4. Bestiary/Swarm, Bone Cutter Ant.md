*Bone Cutter Ants* are 1 ft carnivores that harvest bone, claws, carapace, and other hard materials to build their sprawling surface nests.

Once a suitable corpse is located, they converge on the area, forming long lines to convey their spoils back to the nest. *Bone Cutter Ants* are extremely aggressive, attacking anything they perceive as a threat or potential donor (particularly humanoids in rigid armour).
