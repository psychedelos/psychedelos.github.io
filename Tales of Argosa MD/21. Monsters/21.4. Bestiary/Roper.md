*Ropers* are ten foot stalagmite shaped subterranean monsters, with rock hard hides, lashing tentacles, and a single baleful eye. Their six mucus covered tendrils snatch at targets, dragging them towards their fang filled maw. Ropers are chameleonic, blending into ordinary stalagmites at distances greater than Melee. They have dog like intelligence and are *always* hungry.
