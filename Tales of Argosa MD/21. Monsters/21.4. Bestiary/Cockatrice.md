The cockatrice is a 6 ft, black feathered wyvern like beast with bird legs and a spine crested rooster head. Its bite turns victims to stone. Petrification may be reversed with a ritual involving cockatrice blood or a *[[Flesh to Stone|Flesh to Stone]]* spell.
