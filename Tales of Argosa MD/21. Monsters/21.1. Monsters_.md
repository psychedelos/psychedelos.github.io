### # Number Appearing

The figure or die after the hashtag indicates the usual **number** of **creatures travelling** in the wilds. At the GM's option, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]] (unlikely)* to determine whether there is a **Lair** nearby (with +3d6/+6d8/+100 monsters, or GM's call).

### Armour Class

The monster's Armour Class or **AC.** Represents the creature's tough hide, scales, agility, etc. Humanoid monsters using armour and shields generally apply the equipment rules.

### Hit Dice

Monster Hit Dice or **HD** are the number of **1d8s** rolled to calculate **hit points.** If there is a plus next to the number, add that amount to the hp total. If relevant, a **Monster's "level"** is equal to its HD (and similarly, a classed character's "HD" equals their level).

### Attacks

The number of attacks the monster makes with its action. A monster's **attack bonus is equal to their HD** (eg, a 3 HD monster rolls 1d20+3 to attack vs AC), to a **max of +12** (*[[21.3. Special Traits_#Boss|Bosses]]* may exceed this cap; GM's call). **Multiple** attacks of the same kind are in **parentheses.** Separate rolls are made for each attack. Monster attacks and damage are simplified and do not apply the normal attribute modifiers.

### Nat 19

On a **natural 19** attack roll monsters cause a **special effect**. Humanoids using weapons generally apply the weapon rules. For specific injury effects refer to the *[[13.1.1. Injuries & Setbacks|Injuries & Setbacks]]* or *[[13.2. Trauma Tables_|Trauma]]* tables. Some monsters cause their special effect and/or crits over a wider range, referenced for example as Nat 18-20.

### Attributes

Average attributes are provided for attribute checks and contests. NPC attack, damage, and defence bonuses are simplified, and may not apply attribute modifiers the way PCs do. GM discretion applies.

### Luck

An enemy's *Luck* attribute functions like PC *Luck*, but is not reduced on successful save.

### Movement

Monsters move at **Normal speed** ("Norm") unless noted otherwise. Some creatures might also have a special movement mode (eg fly, burrow, etc).

### Reaction

Each monster has a list of custom **[[11.1.1. Reaction|Reaction]]** results to better reflect the inclinations of that kind of creature, should the GM wish to use them.

### Special Abilities

Special abilities such as spell effects require an **action** to use unless noted otherwise. A number in **parentheses** means the ability may be used up to that quota per encounter, including repeated use of the same ability (GM's call). Eg: an opponent with **Spells (3)** could cast a mix of spells or perhaps the same spell three times. Use the creature's **Int** score for **casting checks** (some stat blocks provide a special Int score for spells). Spells & powers based on **caster level** are **capped at level 10** unless the GM rules otherwise.

### Recharge

Recharge abilities may be used once and not again until the monster's recharge conditions are met. If a percentile check, roll at the start of their turn.

### Harvesting the Dead

Harvesting monsters for venom, parts, etc, requires an **Int (Animal Lore\*, Apothecary\*, etc) check**. Generally one monster may be harvested for one dose of poison/special ingredient (enough to create one item). Whilst not realistic this limit is required for game balance. GM discretion applies.
