Rough AC, *Luck* and Damage expressions appear below (based on **Hit Dice**) to assist with making your own monsters. As always - but **especially** with monsters - GM discretion applies. Naturally lucky or skilled foes might have higher than usual *Luck* for example. If adding multiple attacks, compare with similar HD foes and extrapolate. Referees are encouraged to be **creatively devious** with Nat 19 effects & other abilities.
