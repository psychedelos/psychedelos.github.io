Some special monster traits are grouped together here for ease of reference.

### Boss

A ***Boss*** is a particularly powerful example of an enemy, strong enough to **fight the party alone** or with little back up. The template may be applied to any monster, from vampires and dragons to gang leaders and ogre chiefs. *Bosses* gain the following traits:

- **Quick:** PCs must roll a Great Success on their Initiative check to go before a *Boss*.
- **Double HP:** A *Boss* doubles its hit points. If hp is included in a *Boss* stat block in an adventure, hp has already been adjusted.
- **Off Turn Attacks:** A *Boss* makes *Off Turn Attacks.*
- **Major Exploit Protection:** A *Boss* is immune to Major Exploits until *[[12.11. Conditions_#Wounded|Wounded]]*.
- **Stronger Luck:** *Bosses* may make a *Luck* save to completely negate (or mitigate, GM's call) any adverse spell, ability, exploit, or other effect (even if a save is not normally allowed).
- **Rerolls:** *Bosses* have 1d4+1 *Rerolls* available at the time combat commences. *Boss Rerolls* may be used for *any* of their rolls.

> [!note] Designer's Thoughts
> The *Boss* template is meant to threaten the party with a single, very dangerous foe that breaks the normal rules, capable of challenging the PCs alone.
>
> *Off Turn Attacks* allow a Boss to match the party's action economy, while more hit points, *Stronger Luck* and a small number of *Rerolls* make it difficult for PCs to end the fight early.

### Demons & Undead

***Demons*** are evil and corruption incarnate, utterly depraved and malicious. ***Undead*** are sustained by a cursed half-life, trapped in an unliving but animate state. *Demons* and *Undead* do not sleep, eat, or breathe, and are immune to poison and all mind affecting abilities (including for example charm, fear, illusion, and stun) except madness. All *Demons* and some *Undead* are immune to non magical weapons, excluding **cold iron**. A vial of **holy water** causes them **1d10** damage (whether thrown, applied to a weapon, etc).

### Heavy

A ***Heavy*** is an extra strong example of an enemy type, but something less than a full *Boss*. They have superior equipment as well as the following traits:

- **Quick:** PCs must roll a Great Success on their Initiative check to go before a *Heavy*.
- **More HP:** Increase hp by 50%. If hp is included in a *Heavy* stat block in an adventure, hp has already been adjusted.
- **Stronger Luck:** *Heavies* may make a *Luck* save to completely negate (or mitigate, GM's call) any adverse spell, ability, exploit, or other effect (even if a save is not normally allowed).
- **Major Exploit Protection:** A *Heavy* is immune to Major Exploits until *[[12.11. Conditions_#Wounded|Wounded]]*.

### Immunity to Non Magical Weapons

Immunity to non magical weapons includes natural attacks. At the GM's option, very large creatures, *Bosses*, *Heavies*, and monsters with a supernatural bent bypass this immunity.

### Incorporeal

Incorporeal creatures are visible but intangible, able to fly and pass through objects and creatures like a human moves through water. They are immune to non magical weapons, excluding ***cold iron.***

### Lycanthropes

Lycanthropes are half human, half beast monsters, the classic example being the werewolf. In Argosa all were-creatures are inherently evil, savage and cruel, delighting in eating humans. Lycanthropes may spend an **action** to shift between three forms: **human** (or other standard race), **beast**, and **hybrid**. The racial form tends to have some resemblance to the beast form (eg werewolves appear as very hairy humans with an acute sense of smell).

Lycans have ***Regen*** **(All damage, silver, fire)**, but unlike normal regeneration, their incredible healing continues to function even if the monster is reduced to zero hit points. A character **bitten or scratched** by a lycanthrope must make a *Luck* (Con) save at the end of combat or be **cursed with lycanthropy** themselves. Victims have until the next full moon to have the curse removed (GM's call as to how this might be possible) or the change is permanent. A permanent lycanthrope PC becomes an NPC under the referee's control.

### Magic Resistance %

Enemies with Magic Resistance are unaffected by adverse magical effects if they **roll 1d100** equal to or under the nominated percentage. For area effects, the magic is negated for the protected creature only. A *Demon* caught in a *[[Hellblast|Hellblast]]* might be safe, but other creatures still burn.

### Off Turn Attacks

Some dread foes have *Off Turn Attacks*, allowing them to make a single physical **attack**, or cast a **spell**, after each PC turn during a round (or allied NPC turns, eg hirelings or pets). *Off Turn Attacks* do not trigger if it is also the creature's normal turn in the initiative order. If the monster only has melee attacks, and no adjacent enemies, it may also Move.

> [!example]
> A *Demon* with **Off Turn Attacks** is fighting three characters who win initiative. The Ranger goes, then the *Demon* gets an *Off Turn Attack*, then the hireling goes, then the *Demon* gets an *Off Turn Attack*, then the Fighter goes, and finally the *Demon* gets its normal turn. The round then ends.

### Regeneration

Creatures with *Regeneration* instantly heal the indicated amount of hp at the end of the round. If damaged with the nominated attack type however, any regen is suspended that round. Regen usually ceases to function if the creature is reduced to zero hp (especially if dismembered, burnt, or buried).

### Senses

The GM decides whether a monster can see or otherwise navigate in total darkness. As a general rule, all PC races and most humanoids require light sources, and animals have their usual senses. Some creatures have special senses such as echolocation, tremor sense, body heat, supernatural senses, etc. The GM determines these on a case by case basis.

### Swarms

Some monsters may be treated as a single "swarm" rather than individual monsters (eg: *[[Rat Swarm|Rat Swarm]]*). Swarms **attack all targets in melee range** each round. They suffer half damage from weapons, double damage from area effects, and **split into two** when reduced to **zero hp** (once only, same stats as the original Swarm, but half hp). On a **Nat 19**, the target is overwhelmed by sheer numbers (*[[12.11. Conditions_#Helpless|Helpless]]* for 1d3 rounds; a *Luck* (Str or Dex) save resists).
