Each PC **starts an adventure** with a number of *Rerolls* determined by their level. This is the maximum number of *Rerolls* the character may have at any time:

### Rerolls

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Level 1-3** | *Rerolls* equal to level. |
| **Level 4-6** | *Rerolls* equal to 4. |
| **Level 7-9** | *Rerolls* equal to 5. |

If an adventurer **fails** one of the below checks, or does not obtain as favourable a result as they would like, they may use a *Reroll*.

- **Attribute Check** If the PC has a relevant **skill**, or possibly **background** (see below), or when attempting to activate a **class ability** (eg making a Will check to activate the Monk's *Fork the River* ability).
- ***Luck*** rolls.
- ***[[13.1. Injury & Death_#Death Save|Death saves]]***.
- **Short Rest** Willpower recovery checks.

Only **one** ***Reroll*** may be used **per action**. If advantage or disadvantage applies, only one of the results are rerolled. *Rerolls* may sometimes be spent for other reasons, as noted in individual ability descriptions.

### Backgrounds

If an action is **closely connected** with a character's background, and is **not** a listed **skill**, *Rerolls* might be available for that task (GM's call). For example, a Roof Thatcher background might permit an Int reroll to identify weak points on rooftop.
