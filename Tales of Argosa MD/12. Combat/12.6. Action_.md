### Common Actions

A character may take one action during their turn. An action includes anything that can be done in a few seconds, for example:

- Making an attack.
- Casting a spell.
- Dodging.
- Drinking a potion.
- Activating a magical amulet.
- Sheathing or stowing a weapon.
- Retrieving an item from the ground.

**Picking a lock** or **disarming a trap** usually takes more than one action (even if only one check is required), unless the GM determines otherwise.

When using **Battle Gear**, drawing or otherwise **readying** your item(s) occurs as **part of your action** to attack or use the object. Battle Gear is stored on your person in easily reached places for the purposes of combat.

Accessing **Pack Gear** is not possible in combat unless the GM decides otherwise, based on the circumstances. For example, stuffing a small idol into your pack might be possible as an action.

**Dropping** an item does **not** require an action ("no action"). For example, a warrior armed with a great axe wants to fire his bow. He could spend an action to stow his great axe, and then ready and shoot his bow the following round, or he could drop his axe (no action) and immediately ready and shoot his bow. In this example however his axe is now on the ground, which requires an action to retrieve should he need it later.

Some abilities allow adventurers to take special actions or cause effects **during other character's turns.** Examples include Rescue Exploits and the Bard's *[[5.4. Bard_#Inspire Greatness|Inspire Greatness]]* ability.
