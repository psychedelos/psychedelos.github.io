### Melee Attacks

To attack in melee, you must be in **Melee range** of your opponent or using a **Second Rank** weapon. Make the following attack roll: **1d20 + your Class Attack Bonus for your level + Str modifier.** If the result is equal to or higher than the target's Armour Class (AC), you hit. **Roll damage** per the weapon table + Str modifier.

#### Melee Modifiers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Outnumber Enemies (3:1 or more)** | +1 (+2) attack bonus |
| **Prone Target (or Attacker)** | +2 (-2) attack bonus |
| **Grabbed Target (or Attacker)** | +2 (-2) attack bonus |
| **Near Blind** | 33% miss chance |
| **Blind** | 66% miss chance\* (see [[12.11. Conditions_#Blind\|p.82]]) |

### Ranged Attacks

To attack at range with a bow, sling, thrown dagger or similar, roll: **1d20 + your Class Attack Bonus for your level + Perc modifier.** If the result is equal to or higher than the target's AC you hit. **Roll damage** according to the weapon table + Perc mod.

#### Ranged Modifiers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Shooter in Melee** | Disadvantage |
| **Target in Melee** | Target gains *Half Cover*, and if the attack misses there is a 33% chance of rerolling the attack against an ally in the same melee (if any) |
| **Target in Cover** | - *Half Cover* +2 AC & *Luck* (Dex) saves<br>- *Three Quarters Cover* +4 AC & *Luck* (Dex) saves |
| **Near Blind** | 33% miss chance |
| **Blind** | 66% miss chance\* (see [[12.11. Conditions_#Blind\|p.82]]) |
| **Shooting Beyond Weapon Range** | GM's call (if yes, Disad) |

### Damage

Damage is **deducted** from the target's **hit points.** When an NPC is reduced to zero hit points, they die. When a PC is reduced to zero hit points, they are unconscious or dead. See the [[13.1. Injury & Death_|Injury & Death]] section for more details.

> [!example]
> A 3rd level Fighter with Str 14 has a class attack bonus of 3, and a Str modifier of 1, for a total of +4. He attacks a skeleton with AC 11. On a 1d20 roll of 7 or more (+4, total 11), the skeleton is hit and damage is rolled. If the Fighter was using a longsword, the damage would be 1d8+1 hit points (longsword weapon damage being 1d8, +1 for Str).

### Critical Hits

A critical hit occurs when the 1d20 attack roll is a natural 20. Critical hits by PCs cause **max damage plus half level** (round up). Other characters cause max damage only. Exploding damage dice do not explode on a crit.

### Fumbles

On a natural 1, the attacker has fumbled, somehow exposing themselves to counterattack:

- If the target is in **melee** with the Fumbler, the target gains a free attack against them (choose one attack if the target normally has multiple attacks).
- If the target is **distant** and not in melee, the target immediately returns fire with a readied ranged or thrown weapon (if any).
- If the target is distant and **in melee with an ally**, the Fumbled attack is rerolled against a random ally in the melee.

### Nat 19s

Weapon and monster attacks cause special effects on a natural 19 roll, see [[9.9. Weapons_|p.58]]. If a Nat 19 effect overlaps with a critical hit, apply both.

### Dodge

A character may use their **action** to dodge or otherwise focus on defence. Until the start of their next turn, a dodging character has advantage on Dex checks and *Luck* (Dex) saves to resist adverse effects, and enemies suffer **disadvantage on attack rolls** against them.
