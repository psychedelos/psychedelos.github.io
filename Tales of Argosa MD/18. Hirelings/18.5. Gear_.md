In addition to everyday items, **non-combatant** hirelings may use light armour, one handed melee weapons, and slings. **Roll 1d4** for their starting gear.

**Mercenaries** may use any weapon, light or medium armour, and shields. **Roll 1d12** for their starting gear, plus dagger. Naturally, PCs may buy better equipment for their hirelings.
