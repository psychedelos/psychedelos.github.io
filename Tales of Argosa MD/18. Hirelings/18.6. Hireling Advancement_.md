Hirelings (and perhaps pets) that survive an adventure **roll 2d6** for an advancement. If they evolve into henchmen, they **become 1st level** in an appropriate class, and are loyal to their PC if accepted. If a PC declines a henchmen, or if the henchmen has any outstanding Payback, they depart for adventures of their own.

**Henchmen** gain experience like a PC. Whether they receive a share of party treasure is a matter left to PCs to negotiate (typically a half share).
