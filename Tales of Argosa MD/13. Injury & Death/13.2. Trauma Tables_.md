### Blade Trauma

Blade related injuries and setbacks are caused by Nat 19 rolls from piercing and cutting weapons such as swords, knives, and axes, but also claws and teeth. **Roll 1d12** unless instructed otherwise.

![[13.2.1. Blade Trauma]]

### Blast Trauma

Blast related injuries and setbacks are caused by explosive forces such as *[[Hellblast|Hellblast]]*, powder grenades, ship artillery, dragon breath, and so on. If multiple targets are caught in the area, roll for each character (GMs might prefer to group NPCs). Rolls tend to vary from **1d6 to 1d12** depending on the attack.

![[13.2.2. Blast Trauma]]

### Blunt Trauma

Blunt injuries and related setbacks are caused by bludgeoning weapons such as hammers, fists, tail whips, etc. **Roll 1d12** unless instructed otherwise.

![[13.2.3. Blunt Trauma]]

### Missile Trauma

Missile injuries and related setbacks are caused by arrows, bolts, sling stones, thrown daggers, and so on. Very large missiles use other tables (eg for boulders use the *[[13.2.3. Blunt Trauma|Blunt Trauma]]* table, for explosive catapult ammunition use *[[13.2.2. Blast Trauma|Blast Trauma]]*, etc). **Roll 1d12** unless noted otherwise.

![[13.2.4. Missile Trauma]]

### Toxin Trauma

*[[13.2.5. Toxin Trauma|Toxin Traumas]]* are primarily caused by drugs, noxious fumes, plant spores, and mixing potions. Poisons are covered separately on [[26.4.18. Poison|p.226]]. Unless noted otherwise, *[[13.2.5. Toxin Trauma|Toxin Traumas]]* last twice as long as the original drug, or 1d6 hours, whichever is longer. **Roll 1d12** unless noted otherwise.
