If you prefer to improvise a dungeon adventure from scratch, try these **tables and procedures.** These charts are intended to be used in conjunction with ToA's other tables, oracles, etc.

To begin, **roll 1d20** for the dungeon **Site**:

![[25.4.1. Dungeon Site]]

To determine the **location** of the dungeon site on your campaign hex map:

- Use the party's **current position** or the closest **settlement** as the starting point.
- **Roll 2d6** to generate distance in **hexes**.
- *[[23.3. Read the Signs_|Read the Signs]]* for **direction** and mark the site.

Next **roll 1d20** for the dungeon's **size**:

![[25.4.2. Size]]

Bearing in mind the **size** of the dungeon, choose a **map** from your map collection, do a quick **online** search, or try a map **generator**.

Alternatively, to **create a map** as you go see [[#Playing the Adventure|p.220]]

**Roll 1d6 twice** for the party **Objective**:

![[25.4.3. Objective]]

**Roll 1d20** for the party's **reward**. If the result is a poor fit for your party, roll again.

![[25.4.4. Reward]]

**Roll 1d20** for the **HD** of the **main opposition** of the adventure. Once the HD is established, roll on the **[[21.2.1. Monsters by HD|Monster by HD]]** chart to identify the type of creature. If the result is a **solitary** creature, either **reroll** or also **roll 1d6** for a **secondary** faction as the main opposition (they might be minions, enemies, neutral neighbours, etc).

![[25.4.5. Main Opposition]]

**Roll 1d20** for a **Special Leader** (possibly a *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Boss|Boss]]*) or simply make one of the main opposition a *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Boss|Boss]]*.

![[25.4.6. Special Leader]]

Next, *[[23.3. Read the Signs_|Read the Signs]]* for guidance about the Leader's **motivation** & **status quo** of the dungeon. Then considering all of the information generated so far, **formulate a snapshot** of the current situation, crystalise any **required details**, and decide on a **start point** for the PCs.

### Playing the Adventure

Play the adventure using all of ToA's normal rules, including **oracles** for inspiration and guidance as desired. For **doors** and entryways, **roll 1d20**:

![[25.4.7. Doors]]

If you have a **pre-made map** skip the below table. If you're **generating** the map on the fly, **roll 1d20** to flesh out the complex as you go:

![[25.4.8. Rooms & Corridors]]

- **Rooms** have **1d4 exits** (the first room has at least two). Place them wherever makes sense. You may decide some rooms only have one exit (eg a vault). As the map unfolds, corridor connections may form with prior locations.
- If you have a **string** of corridors or rooms, or if a result is **impossible** (eg due to space), reroll. Caverns generally won't have doors but may have rubble, sink holes, etc.
- If all paths are **dead ends**, place **secret door** or hatch in the most appropriate location. If the PCs reach the **final room** (based on dungeon size) and haven't found or achieved their **objective**, their goal is in the final room.
- To **leave the dungeon**, exit via the entrance, or make a *Luck* save at the next stairs or hatch.

When **entering** a room, **roll 1d20** for its **Theme** and **interpret** consistent with the dungeon **Site**:

![[25.4.9. Room Theme]]

**Roll 1d20** for a **room's contents**.

- If you have a **string** of the **same results**, reroll or use #16-18 (2d6 opposition) instead.
- If seeking a certain **thing**, and an appropriate room is found, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]]* to see if the thing is present (probably with the **Leader**).
- Standard **[[27.1. Treasure_|treasure]]** rules apply. If unsure *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]]* (any modifiers GM's call).

![[25.4.10. Room Contents]]

If desired, **roll 1d20** below for ideas about the **kinds of items** located as part of a room's contents.

![[25.4.11. Theme Items]]

If a location has a **Complication, roll 1d20** and **interpret** the result based on the dungeon **Site** and room **Theme**, and/or *[[23.3. Read the Signs_|Read the Signs]]* for guidance.
