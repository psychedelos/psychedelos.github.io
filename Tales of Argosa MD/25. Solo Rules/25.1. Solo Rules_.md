*Tales of Argosa* is well suited to **Solo play.** The many random tables, as well as the Dice and Deck Oracles, Reaction rolls, exploration procedures, Nat 19 effects, Chases, etc, allow the player to cede many traditional GM decisions to chance, fostering a more independent and emergent world.

If solo gaming with a **party of three or more PCs,** the game will function as usual and no special adjustments are required. For a **party of two,** you might consider reducing the number of enemies encountered by a third, or hiring some NPCs to beef up your daring duo.
