| 1 | **Water** The area might be flooded, contains a pool, underground lake, well, fountain, broken pipes, and so on. |
| 2 | **Ambush** Something is lying in wait for the PCs. Roll for a [[17.5. Encounter Tables_\|Random Encounter]] for creature type. |
| 3 | **Trap** There is a trap nearby [[26.1. Traps_\|p.222]]. |
| 4 | **Magic** A magical effect is present; ward, enchantment, illusion, etc. *[[23.3. Read the Signs_\|Read the Signs]]* for guidance. |
| 5 | **Toxin** Something poisonous is here; mould spores, noxious gas, bad food, a poison needle trap, etc. |
| 6 | **Blocked** The way forward is blocked or impeded in some way; rubble, water, fire, portcullis, netting, chasm, etc. |
| 7 | **Foulness** slime, god awful stench, rotting body or debris, noxious mould, etc. Possible disease or parasites. |
| 8 | **Lighting** here is opposite to most of the dungeon. Perhaps glow bugs underground or no windows in a tower, etc. |
| 9 | **Boon** something helpful or beneficial is nearby; an item, loot, creature, or perhaps the room itself is a safe space. |
| 10 | **Puzzle** there is some manner of puzzle, game, or riddle to be solved. |
| 11 | **Confined** something is caged, confined, or otherwise restrained here or nearby. |
| 12 | **Obscured** sight or hearing is obscured; fog, smoke, loud machinery, whistling winds, pillars, statues, tall bookshelves, cluttered tables, piles of junk, etc. |
| 13 | **Unstable** the chamber itself or something within is unstable, slippery, unsteady, subject to tremors, teetering, etc. |
| 14 | **Fire** something is burning, melting, or heating up. Alternatively the remains of such, eg charred bones, piles of ash, smoke stains on the ceiling, charcoal, etc. |
| 15 | **Vermin** are present in large numbers; rats, bats, cockroaches, snakes, mosquitoes, etc. Possible disease or parasites. |
