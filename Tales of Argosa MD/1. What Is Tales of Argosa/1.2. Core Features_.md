ToA is d20 based, with the following features:

- **9 Classes** Artificer, Barbarian, Bard, Cultist, Fighter, Monk, Ranger, Rogue & Magic User; only two of which involve magic.
- **9th Level max** caps PC power levels.
- **Flatter HP Curve** Most PCs will have approx 10-40 hit points over their nine levels.
- **Unique Features** Every 3rd level design your own PC ability in conjunction with your GM, or choose from 40+ examples.
- **Roll Under** for attribute checks, making every stat point matter (inc degrees of success).
- **Skills** allow access to a PC's limited *Rerolls* if they fail a relevant attribute check.
- **Willpower & Perception** attributes replace Wisdom. Ranged attacks use Perc not Dex.
- **Minor, Major & Rescue Exploits** on top of damage, not in lieu, promote improvised stunts and moments of greatness.
- **Fast Initiative** Only one player rolls for the whole party each round, aiming for equal to or under their Initiative score (average of Dex and Int). If they succeed the party goes first, otherwise the monsters do.
- **Diminishing Luck** attribute replaces saving throws, ratcheting up danger with each save.
- **Fierce Battle** Martial Exploits, Nat 19 effects, Crits, Fumble ripostes, Morale checks, and Trauma rolls make for action packed battles! When reduced to zero hp, healing takes 1d3 minutes (magical or otherwise). Players don't roll to find out if their fallen PC lives until after combat, when someone checks the body.
- **Unbalanced Encounters** ToA is designed for unbalanced combat. Clear escape rules put the fate of the PCs in the players' hands - do they fight another round, or run?
- **Short Rests** PCs may attempt Will checks to recover hp and class abilities, encouraging the party to press on rather than camp.
- **Dark & Dangerous Magic** (DDM) threatens to unleash Veil monsters, madness, mutation, or worse! Meddling with sorcery is not for the faint of heart.
- **Downtime** rules for Carousing, Pets, Black Market Trade, Masterforged Gear, Gambling, Recovery, Potion Brewing, Magical Research, Scroll Inscribing, Rumours, and more.
- **Emergent Play Support for Group & Solo Play** via mechanics, procedures, and the GM Toolbox, including: Range Bands, Reaction & Activity rolls, Morale, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]]* Dice Oracle, *[[23.3. Read the Signs_|Read the Signs]]* Card Deck Oracle, Hexploration Procedures, Dungeon Events, Travel Events, Random Encounters, XP Awards, Treasure Tables, Weather, Hirelings, NPC tables, Diseases & Parasites, Madness, Traps, and more!
- **Creative Commons Licence**.
- **Rules as Guidelines** The GM is the final authority on all rules, which are expected to be tweaked to fit table preferences (the [[28.1. Variant Rules_|Variant Rules]] chapter includes suggestions).
