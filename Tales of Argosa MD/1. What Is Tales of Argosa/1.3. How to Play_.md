The basics of playing a tabletop adventure game are very simple. One player is known as the Games Master ("GM") or Referee; this player runs the overall game in an impartial manner, playing all the characters the other players don't (the Non Player Characters or "NPCs"). For example, the GM plays the world's monsters, merchants, inn keepers, queens, and evil sorcerers.

The other players control a single adventurer each, also known as a Player Character or "PC"; a Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Bard, Ranger, Monk, Artificer, Cultist or Magic User. The player characters are the central protagonists in the game, like the main characters in a novel, TV show or movie. The players might also control NPC hirelings and pets (if any).

The game generally unfolds with the GM describing the situation at hand, including where the adventurers are and what they can see and hear. The players then tell the GM what they want their PCs to do. The GM decides whether the attempted action succeeds, fails, or if dice need to be rolled to determine the outcome.

An example of play appears below.

**GM (Steve):** Okay, as you stroll down the alley way, you hear a sudden squeal, then a body falls from the sky above you, landing on the cobblestones with a bloody thud!

**Tom:** What? A body? Where the hell did it come from?

**Jessica:** Is the guy dead? Is it a man or woman? What are they wearing?

**GM:** The inn beside you is two storeys high. Your torchlight just reaches the rooftop; you can make out two humanoid shapes withdrawing from the roof's edge.

**Tom:** Hold up - as soon as I see the humanoids on the roof, I throw my spear at one.

**GM:** OK, so *Kruncor* is throwing his spear. Tom, make an Initiative check to see if *Kruncor* reacts fast enough before the figures disappear. Jessica, to answer your questions; the body is a man, in wealthy merchant robes of red silk. Definitely dead. You notice he's missing three fingers on his left hand.

**Mia:** I crouch next to the body and search it. Anything good?

**Jessica:** Hey I was about to do that - we split whatever she finds!

**Tom:** OK I made my Initiative check with an 8, so I react quick enough. I throw my spear, yelling out "What the hell, berk!" ... let's see \<Tom rolls his attack and damage> ... I hit AC 18, and damage is 8, assuming that's a hit?

**GM:** Yep you easily hit, even with cover \<the GM knows the target's AC is 12>. Your spear strikes one of the figures, who yells out in pain... \<the GM rolls a Dex check, which fails> .., and he topples off the roof, hitting the ground with a horrible splat!

**Tom:** LOL! Classic *Kruncor!*

**Jessica:** Yeah that's the *Kruncor* we all know and love. Right, I loot the new dead guy!

**GM:** OK then; Mia - you don't find anything of much value on the dead merchant. Interestingly his money pouch has been cut from his belt. Jessica, the latest dead body is a man wearing street rags, there's a bloody knife, 3 silver in loose coins and a money pouch with another 82 silver.

**Jessica:** Haha oh yeah!

**Mia:** We're splitting that!

**GM:** Tom - what does *Kruncor* do while *Dari* and *Yoroch* are looting?

**Tom:** Can I climb up the side of the inn?

**GM:** Of course you can. If you want to do it in a hurry though you'll need to make either a Strength or Dex check, your choice. The Athletics skill applies if you have it.

**Tom:** Great, I'll go Strength \<rolls 1d20 and gets 19, but *Kruncor's* Strength is 16, so the check is a fail>. Ah damn it. But I've got the Athletics skill, so I can use one of my Rerolls - let's see, it's a 5 - Great Success! (the 5 is a Great Success because it is less than half *Kruncor's* strength score of 16).

**GM:** Sweet! *Kruncor* scales the side of the inn like a monkey, leaping from one windowsill to another then hauling himself up onto the tiled roof. You see a one eyed female in street rags, menacing you with an iron baton. She hisses at you in Argosan: *"New to these parts, is ya? I'm with the ..."*

**Tom:** I grab her and throw her off the roof.

**GM:** What?

**Tom:** I grab her as she's talking, and fling her off the roof down to where *Dari* and *Yoroch* are waiting.

**Mia:** Yep, *Dari* stands up and draws her short swords, ready for trouble. She knows what *Krunc's* like.

**GM:** OK. Someone roll initiative, we're about to start combat.

### Game Dice

ToA uses several kinds of dice, identified by how many sides they have. For example, a single six sided die is referred to as "**1d6**", while 4d6 means roll four six sided dice and total the result. The four sided die is "**1d4**", eight sided die is "**1d8**", ten-sided die is "**1d10**", twelve sided die is "**1d12**", and the twenty sided die is "**1d20**".

If you need to roll "**1d100**", roll 1d10 twice using the first throw as the tens and the second throw as the ones (eg if you rolled 3 and 9, the result would be a 39). **1d66** is like 1d100 but uses two d6s instead. For **1d2** roll 1d4 and divide by two (round up), and finally for **1d3** roll 1d6 and divide by two (round up).

#### Exploding Dice

An "exploding" die roll means that if you roll the maximum on the die, you roll it again and accumulate the result (once per die only).

### Round Down

If a rule instructs you to divide an amount, round down unless indicated otherwise.
