Tales of Argosa is a Sword & Sorcery inspired **Emergent Play Adventure Game**, for groups or solo. Adventures are short, sharp, and focused on dangerous wilds, treacherous cities, fierce battles, ruinous magic, fabulous treasures, & cosmic weird.

### Simple Rules

ToA has **simple** rules, **flexible** PC customisation, improvisation **support**, and an emphasis on **GM rulings.** It's a mix of old school, modern, and new game design, wrapped around a familiar **d20 core**.

### Fierce Battle

Battle is **fast, brutal,** & **exciting!** Any sword swing might lead to fractured bones or severed limbs, and the **spectre of death** is ever present. **Retreat** rules allow the PCs to decide whether to fight or run!

### Dread Sorcery

Argosan sorcery is not only rare, it is **dark** and **inherently dangerous.** Be it arcane spell or divine blessing, magic is a power not meant for mortals; those who engage with it do so at their **peril**.

### Bold Adventurers

ToA scenarios focus on personal contests against savage **wilderness,** treacherous **cities,** murderous monsters, and mysterious **ruins.** PCs are not epic heroes charged with saving the world; they're sword swinging, skull splitting **adventurers** after **fortune** and **glory!**

### Emergent Play

Argosa is designed for **short, sharp** adventures in a **sandbox** world. The GM offers rumours and hooks, but the **players decide** where to go and what to do: which tales to investigate, factions to interact with, places to explore, etc. ToA's mechanics, procedures, tables, and oracles **equip the GM** with a plethora of **support tools** to adapt to player choices - ready to adjudicate any situation or side trek with aplomb.

### Adventure Game

ToA is about **great gameplay** moments with friends or solo. Like many games, an **impartial referee** is required, in this instance between the PCs and the setting. This is facilitated by **rolling in the open**, demonstrating that the **risks** are real and the **world independent** of the GM. This approach also allows everyone to be **surprised** by what unfolds, including (and especially) the referee. From this meeting of **wits & chance** will spring astonishing adventures, fond memories, and lasting friendships. Enjoy!
