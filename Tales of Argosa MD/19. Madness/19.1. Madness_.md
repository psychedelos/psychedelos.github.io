Some things are so shocking, such an affront to the natural order, that they threaten the minds of those who experience them. Grotesque monstrosities, impossible aberrations, depraved demons, and forbidden lore are just a few of the unhinging horrors adventurers might endure. The following rules are intended to add fun to the game, twisting the PC's usual outlook with strange and possibly dangerous eccentricities. If your table is not interested in such things, feel free to ignore.

### Symptoms of Madness

When a character's sanity is threatened, a ***Luck*** **(Will) save** is usually permitted to resist. On a failure the adventurer develops one or more symptoms of Madness. The table below provides a range of examples, leaving the GM and/or player free to elaborate on the details and cause.

### Acute Episodes

As a rule of thumb, an adventurer's Madness should manifest an acute episode at least **once per adventure** (more if the PC has multiple Madness symptoms; GM's call). Players will often be able to improvise their own issues via roleplaying (and gain [[16.2. Advancement_#Social|social xp]]), but in some cases the GM might like to impose a specific complication. *Luck* saves or attribute checks may be permitted to resist or mitigate the severity of an episode (GM's call).

### Curing Madness

Madness cannot be cured by magic, but can be removed by spending time in [[16.8. Recovery_#Madness Remission|Recovery]] during Downtime.
