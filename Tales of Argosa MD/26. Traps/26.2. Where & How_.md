**Spotting** hazards before they go off, and figuring out how to **neutralise** them, is an important part of the trap experience. GMs are encouraged to ask questions of the players about where and how they are searching (or disarming) before:

1. **Check or No Check?** Deciding whether a check is required, and/or
2. **Modifiers** Whether any modifiers ought to apply (similar to the approach adopted for social skills, if using them).

If traps are allowed to devolve into a series of dice rolls without any real player input, they quickly become dull resource sinks instead of interesting hazards.
