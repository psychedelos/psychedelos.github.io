A series of tables appear below to improvise simple traps on the fly. Roll once for each of the following:

1. **Danger** level,
2. **Triggers & Counters**, and
3. **Mode** of attack.

### Danger Level

**Roll 2d6** to determine the trap's danger level (or choose) as appropriate for the situation:

![[26.4.1. Danger Level]]

### Triggers & Counters

Triggers and countermeasures are partly dependent on the nature of the trapped item or space:

- **Portable container** traps activate if opened, moved, or otherwise interacted with, as the referee determines.
- **Roll 2d6** for **spaces** (doors, rooms, hallways, etc) or **large objects** (statues, bookshelves, beds, etc). If a particular trigger doesn't make sense for the situation, roll again (or choose).

![[26.4.2. Triggers & Counters]]

### Mode of Attack

To determine a trap's attack mode, **roll 1d8** for **portable containers** (chests, boxes, jars, etc), or **1d20** for **spaces** and **large objects** (doors, hallways, rooms, etc). If an attack mode does not fit the situation at hand, reroll (or choose).

![[26.4.3. Mode of Attack]]

### Bypasses

Some traps will include a method to safely bypass the hazard to allow passage/access by friendlies. Finding the bypass generally requires searching in a specific location, and/or a Perc (Detection) check (often modified). Prying the information from a captured enemy also works. **Roll 2d6** (or choose) to determine the bypass:

![[26.4.4. Bypasses]]

### Purchasing Traps

Traps are generally categorised as "rare" goods. Rough costs are outlined below according to danger level. Final prices and availability are subject to GM discretion. Most prices will need tweaking to account for the vendor and trap specifics.

#### Cost

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Minor** | 5d10 + 50 sp. |
| **Moderate** | 500 sp + (1d6 x 100 sp). |
| **Major** | 1,500 sp + (2d6 x 100 sp). |
