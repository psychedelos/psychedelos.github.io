### Powers

To determine how many powers a magic item has **roll 2d6** and see below. As usual, the owner must **attune** to the item to access its powers, and using a power requires an **action** unless noted otherwise.

![[27.10.5. Number of Powers]]

**Powers** may be **Obvious** or **Discreet**. **Roll 1d100** to determine which power(s). Not all powers will suit all items, reroll if necessary.

![[27.10.6. Obvious Powers]]

![[27.10.7. Discreet Powers]]

> [!note] Side Effects
> If desired, *[[23.3. Read the Signs_|Read the Signs]]* to spark a themed side effect for the item, or try rolling on the **[[19.1.1. Madness|Madness]]** or **[[13.2.5. Toxin Trauma|Toxin Trauma]]** tables. Apply the side effect once per adventure (as an Acute Episode) or as the GM determines.
