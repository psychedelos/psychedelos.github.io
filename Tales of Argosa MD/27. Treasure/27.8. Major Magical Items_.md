In Argosa, permanent magic items are relatively rare and each item is a unique piece. This is partly due to the scarcity of spell casters (and other sources of power capable of creating them), and partly because the process is long and arduous.

On the one hand, whilst permanent magic items are infrequently made, once created, they are incredibly difficult to destroy, and often endure for hundreds if not thousands of years. An adventurer stumbling across an enchanted blade is likely to find one fashioned in the style of a past age rather than the present. Generally speaking, a permanent magical item cannot be destroyed by ordinary means (though they can be damaged). The GM decides how a particular item might be destroyed.

### Appearance & History

Each magic item has its own special appearance or feel, and a unique history. An enchanted object need not be flashy or made of expensive materials, but anyone who wields it understands there is something different about it. Perhaps a sword never dulls, a gnarled staff is warm to the touch, or a breastplate moulds itself to fit the wearer perfectly. Items such as these endure over generations, developing histories of their own as they pass from one keeper to the next.
