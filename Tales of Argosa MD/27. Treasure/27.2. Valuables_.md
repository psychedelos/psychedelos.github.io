### Valuables A

*[[27.2.1. Valuables A|Valuables A]]* captures lesser art objects (10 - 360 sp), lesser gems (50 - 500 sp), potions, and scrolls. Also a limited chance of a Minor Charm.

### Valuables B

*[[27.2.2. Valuables B|Valuables B]]* covers major art objects (400 - 1000 sp), greater gems (600 - 1500 sp), potions, scrolls, and Minor Charms. There is also a small chance of a Major Magical Item.
