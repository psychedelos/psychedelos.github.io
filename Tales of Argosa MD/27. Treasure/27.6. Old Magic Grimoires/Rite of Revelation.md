**Range:** Self  
**Duration:** Special

Over the course of a lengthy research period (5d20 days), you employ frequent divinations to obtain detailed knowledge of a person, place, or thing. There is a 50% chance of gaining access to particularly important secrets such as hidden lairs, concealed crimes, true identities, true names, or prophecies. At the referee's discretion, certain information may be cryptic in nature (on a Great Success, at least half the information must be clear). This spell cannot be repeated about the same topic. Probing the Veil for answers is not without risk; when the rite is complete you suffer a random Madness.
