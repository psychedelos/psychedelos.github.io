**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** Instant

This spell regrows one lost eye, ear, limb or similar appendage over 2d6 days (a Great Success halves the time). Regenerating lost body parts is taxing on the recipient, permanently reducing their Con by 1d2 points. The Veil also demands a price from the caster; draining them 1d4 *Luck,* which cannot be recovered until the end of their *next* adventure.
