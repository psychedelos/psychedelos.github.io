**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** Instant

All creatures in a Close area with HD equal to half your level or less, rounded up (on a Great Success, half level rounded up, +2) are instantly slain (no *Luck* save permitted). You may choose to exclude eligible targets if you are aware of them. Harvesting souls for the Veil with this dread incanter is both dangerous and sanity fraying; you trigger a DDM effect at disad, lose 1 point of *Luck*, and suffer a random Madness.
