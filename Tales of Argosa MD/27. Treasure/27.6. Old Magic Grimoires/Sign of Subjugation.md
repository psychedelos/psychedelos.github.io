**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** 2d6 rounds

You seize control of a humanoid creature's mind, and may command them by spending an action. The target must obey, even if the action is obviously suicidal. A *Luck* (Will) save resists. On a Great Success, you may affect other kinds of monsters (excluding *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Boss|Bosses]]*). The sorcery required to puppeteer another is particularly taxing, draining you 1 *Luck* and 1d6 Con. Lost Con cannot be restored until your next Downtime period.
