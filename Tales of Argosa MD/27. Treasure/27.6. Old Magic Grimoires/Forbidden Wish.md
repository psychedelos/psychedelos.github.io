**Range:** Very Far  
**Duration:** Instant

This greater sorcery grants you a single wish by beseeching a mysterious entity from beyond the Veil. The GM decides what is possible (a Great Success allows for more) and a bargain is inevitably struck between yourself and the entity that answers. Invoking a *Forbidden Wish* is both taxing and dangerous, triggering a DDM effect at disadvantage and draining 1 point *Luck* permanently (cannot be recovered by any means). The bargain may impose further costs.
