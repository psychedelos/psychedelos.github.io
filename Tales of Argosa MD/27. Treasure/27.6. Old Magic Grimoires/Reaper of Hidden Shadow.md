**Range:** Melee range  
**Duration:** Special

During a ritual spanning dusk till dawn, you summon an *[[Invisible Stalker|Invisible Stalker]]*, an unseen assassin from beyond the Veil. The *Stalker* attempts to perform one task as ordered by you, regardless of how long it may take or how far the *Stalker* must travel. The magic binding the monster is especially strong and imposes disadvantage on any *[[Sever Arcarnum|Sever Arcarnum]]* test.

The *Stalker* relentlessly pursues its objective until completed, the mission becomes impossible, or the *Stalker* is destroyed. If the mission is completed there is a 50% chance the assassin breaks free of your control and attempts to return and kill you (on a *Great Success* it does not return). Otherwise it vanishes forever. Calling forth an *[[Invisible Stalker|Invisible Stalker]]* is both taxing and dangerous, triggering a DDM effect and permanently draining 1 point *Luck* which cannot be recovered by any means.
