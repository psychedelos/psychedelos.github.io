*Morg Tor*, the *Grudge Hammer*, is made of mithral and gold, mined from the purest veins in the earth's deep mantle. Forged by an unnamed dwarven master smith, it is said that *Morg Tor's* perfection was such that the *First Thane* imbued it with his divine favour.

### Morg Tor

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | If you suffer a critical hit, and your next action is to strike your attacker with *Morg Tor*, it becomes a critical hit. |
| **2nd** | Once per week, throw the hammer along with yourself up to Very Far range. If an attack, roll to hit as normal; if successful the target is also *[[12.11. Conditions_#Stunned\|Stunned]]*. On a miss, you scatter within a Melee area of the target (use 1d8 for compass direction). Hit or miss, you roll to their feet, weapon ready. |
| **3rd** | Once per six months, when an ally within Close range is reduced to zero hp, you may bellow an oath of vengeance against your ally's vanquisher. If *Morg Tor* strikes that target with your next action, it causes 50 damage (if reduced to zero hp, the target is destroyed in spectacular fashion). If you miss, roll on the *[[15.6.1. Divine Rebuke\|Divine Rebuke]]* table instead. |
| **Special** | You develop a thick, lustrous beard that cannot be shaved, cut, or burnt away. Dwarves inexplicably find themselves swayed by your words (advantage on social checks). The *Grudge Hammer* is indestructible and cannot be damaged by anything short of a demigod. |
