This fine cooper brooch is fashioned in the likeness of a cockroach, decorated with small bloodstones. Legend tells that the demon *Neb-Shr-Gmog, Tiller of Plagues,* conjured the brooch for a desperate mortal; a "gift" that is part boon, part curse.

### Plague Demon's Clasp

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once every 1d4 days, transform into a cockroach (non-flying variety, treat as *[[Transmogrification\|Transmogrification]]* at 8th level). |
| **2nd** | Once per week, you may infect a touched creature or object with a disease or parasite of your choosing (no initial save). You are immune. |
| **3rd** | Once per month, cause a horde of biting, stinging insects to surge out of your mouth: Melee range, target suffers 12d6 damage, *Luck* (Con) save for half. This power triggers a DDM effect, and boils spread over your whole body for a week. |
| **Special** | You develop boils in a random location, 1d8: (i) face, (ii) neck, (iii) torso, (iv) hand, (v) arm, (vi) foot, (vii) leg, (viii) genitals. The boils are incurable as long as you have the clasp. Anyone physically touching you risks contracting the boils (*Luck* (Con) save resists). |
