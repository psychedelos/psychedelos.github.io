This translucent orb appears to be filled with roiling green fog. Enchanted by the serpentman *T'Seroskk* in a past age, the orb and its master became synonymous with power at any cost. Both were lost in a terrible battle against the *[[Cyclops|Cyclopes]]* in the *Sunstone Ranges.*

### Orb of T'Seroskk

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once per week, brandish the orb to trap a creature within Close range inside it (*Luck* (Will) save resists). Only one creature may be trapped inside the orb at a time. You may later spend an action to release the creature, which obeys your commands (excluding obviously suicidal orders) for 2d6 minutes, then breaks free. |
| **2nd** | Once per week, you may whisper a single suggestion to the orb, which creatures within Close range are compelled to carry out (excluding obviously dangerous suggestions). |
| **3rd** | Once every six months, sacrifice a living humanoid to the orb to gain a *[[Forbidden Wish\|Forbidden Wish]].* Using this power instantly ages you 2d10 years. |
| **Special** | After retaining the orb for more than a month, you feel part of your life force slipping away, siphoned by the *Nest Beyond the Stars* (age 2d10 years). |
