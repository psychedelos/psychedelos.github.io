This exquisite bronze breastplate may be worn as part of medium or heavy armour, and is emblazoned with fire and flame iconography. Originally forged for mercenary captain *Norcin the Cruel*, the armour eventually made its way to Argosa, only to be lost at the infamous *Argos Plateau* massacre.

### Phoenix Armour

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once every 1d4 days, wreathe your weapon in flame (no action) for three rounds. On a hit, targets must make a *Luck* (Dex) save or are *[[12.11. Conditions_#Burning\|Burning]]*. |
| **2nd** | Once per week, you manifest wings of fire (as *[[Wings of the Raven King\|Wings of the Raven King]]* at 7th level but lasts 2d6 rounds). |
| **3rd** | Once only, when reduced to zero hp, you may erupt into white hot flames (4d6 damage to adjacent enemies). At the end of the next round you rise from the ashes at maximum hp, and regain 3 *Luck*, 3 uses of expended class abilities, and 3 *Rerolls*. After using this power, the armour vanishes. |
| **Special** | You develop pyromania (treat as a Madness, but incurable) and are immune to fire. |
