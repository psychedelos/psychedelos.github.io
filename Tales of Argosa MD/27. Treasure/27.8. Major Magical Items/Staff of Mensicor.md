This dread staff is topped with a flesh sack of bulging eyes, toothy maws, and stringy tentacles (dead but preserved). Enchanted by *Mensicor*, a deep witch of the distant past, how the staff made its way to the surface remains a mystery.

### Staff of Mensicor

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | You develop rudimentary dark vision, able to make out rough shapes in complete darkness (disad on Perception checks, *[[12.11. Conditions_#Near Blind\|Near Blind]]* instead of *[[12.11. Conditions_#Blind\|Blind]]*). |
| **2nd** | Once every 1d4 days, strike the staff on the ground to cause alien tendrils to burst upwards within Close range. Enemies within a Melee area are *[[12.11. Conditions_#Grabbed\|Grabbed]]* (Str 19). The rubbery tendrils have AC 14 and 13 hp. They last 2d6 minutes before sinking into the earth. |
| **3rd** | Once per month, conjure a *[[Gibbering Terror\|Gibbering Terror]]* from the void. The *Terror* obeys your commands (no action) and remains for 2d6 rounds or until destroyed. |
| **Special** | You become sensitive to sunlight (-2 penalty to Athletics checks and Rest checks if sunny and you are not inside, underground, or under some other thick cover (forest canopy, etc). |
