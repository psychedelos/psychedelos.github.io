The *Huntress of Men* is a black yew bow with a static recurve, gifted to the elves by the *Bruhga of Still Dreams.* Made for a ranger of the First Age, the bow has taken the lives of countless humanoids, humans foremost among them.

### Cyldimere

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once every 1d4 days, fire an elemental arrow (fire, lightning, frost, etc) causing 5d6 extra damage on a hit, and possibly causing an elemental effect (GM's call). |
| **2nd** | Arrows fired from *Cyldimere* seek the hearts of men (Crit 18-20 vs humanoids). |
| **3rd** | Once per week, you may commune with a tree or plant you touch for 2d6 rounds (use *[[Echo of Days\|Echo of Days]]* at 7th level as a guide). |
| **Special** | Over time you become a strict vegetarian and no longer sleep, instead entering a restful trance (as elf). As a relic of the Unseelie realm, *Cyldimere* cannot be drawn against an elf, fey, or natural beast. |
