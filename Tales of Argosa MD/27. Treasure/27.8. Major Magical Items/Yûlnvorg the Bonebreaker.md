This preternaturally heavy, spiked mace has a bound leather grip. Fashioned of highly rare meteorite ore (also known as *moonsteel*), *Yûlnvorg* was enchanted by *Nocratha the Stargazer* as a reward for one of his first and most loyal disciples. The weapon was lost during the (short lived) *Northgate* rebellion more than a decade ago.
