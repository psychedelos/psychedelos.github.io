This compact coil of pasty coloured, grey striped rope stretches up to 60 ft long, wrapped at both ends. Fashioned from the silk of a giant silkworm, the rope was enchanted by a long dead skorn exile. Imbued with the beastman's loathing for "soft" races, the rope is a potent weapon and tool, but difficult to control.

### Coil of the Worm

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | The rope cannot be severed or burnt. Extremely heavy loads, or creatures with Str 19+ may "stretch" the rope (eg to break free of it), but will not sever it. |
| **2nd** | Once every 1d4 days, you may animate and command the rope for 1d6 minutes (as *[[Infuse Animus\|Infuse Animus]]* at 8th level). |
| **3rd** | Once only, transform the rope into a *[[Purple Worm\|Purple Worm]]*. The worm is ravenous and requires an action and Will check each round to direct it, otherwise it becomes uncontrolled for the duration. The transformation lasts 1d6 x 10 mins, then this power falls dormant. |
| **Special** | When invoking any of the rope's powers, those lacking *[[Skorn\|Skorn]]* blood must make a Will check. If failed, the rope refuses to co-operate, instead triggering a DDM effect. |
