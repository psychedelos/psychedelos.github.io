This gold gilded sword has a red grip and is inset with red opals. Forged at the dawn of the current Age for a knight of the *Iron God* (*[[Graxus (The Iron God)|Graxus]]*, God of war, glory, courage, struggle), the weapon radiates a holy aura if examined with *[[Pierce the Veil|Pierce the Veil]].*

### Ballard's Blade

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | *Ballard's Blade* counts as holy, causing an additional 1d8 damage vs *[[Demons\|Demons]]* and *Undead*. You may cause the sword to glow at will (no action, as candle). |
| **2nd** | While you have the blade drawn, gain 50% Magic Resistance. |
| **3rd** | Once per month, forcibly present the sword as a holy symbol to banish a supernatural creature within Close range to the Void (*Luck* (Will) save negates). |
| **Special** | You develop a hunger for glory, so much so that the GM may call for an occasional Will check to resist opportunities for renown (treat similar to a Madness, but cannot be cured). |
