For a Group check, the whole party succeeds as long as **more than half the group succeeds.** If half succeed and half fail, the GM might allow a Great Success or Terrible Failure to decide, or require a failing PC to make a *Luck* save for the group to pass. Modifiers may apply as usual. Unless the GM decides otherwise, **all PCs must roll** for a Group check, even if more than half the company have already succeeded or failed.
