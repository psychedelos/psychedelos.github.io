If two characters are in direct contest, both characters roll and **whoever succeeds by the greatest margin wins**. Ties mean the status quo remains, or might require a reroll at the GM's discretion.

Contests might involve the same kind of attribute roll (eg: opposed Str checks when arm wrestling), or different attribute rolls (eg: a Dex (Stealth) vs Perc (Detection) check when an adventurer tries to hide from a town guard).
