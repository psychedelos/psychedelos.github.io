From time to time the GM may rule that a simple random check, eg a **d100 percentage, x-in-6, or** ***[[23.2. Consult the Bones_|Consult the Bones]]*** **roll** will resolve the uncertainty of a situation. For example, with a *[[Basilisk|Basilisk]]* surrounded by three PCs, the GM decides the beast will attack *Kruncor* on a d6 roll of 1-2, *Lyssa* on a 3-4, or their poor hireling *Ratsack* on a 5-6. Additionally, certain powerful monsters may have percentile based abilities, such as *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Magic Resistance %|Magic Resistance]].*
