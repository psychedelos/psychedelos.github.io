### Standard Research

Standard research covers just about any non magical topic the PCs are interested in, including dungeon locations, clues to the whereabouts of a magic item, monster ecology or vulnerabilities, relevant histories, etc.

**Basic** research generally takes **1d6 days** and costs **5 sp/day**. More **Advanced** research requires **1d4+2 weeks** and costs **100 sp/week** (or more, reflecting library fees, research assistants, sage consultations, and so on). The GM decides what qualifies as basic or advanced research consistent with the world.

After the research period, an **Int check** is required (either by the PC or hired NPC; various **Lore** or **Gather Information** skills might apply). In some instances, the GM might prefer to make this roll in secret on behalf of the players. The table below provides guidance as to the results.

### Magical Research

Magical research includes such things as **learning a new spell**, deciphering **how to use** the powers of a magic item, or **how to break** a wizard's ward.

For the creation of permanent magic items or effects, see **[[15.10. Ritual Magic_|Ritual Magic]]**. For **[[16.7. Potion Brewing_|Potion Brewing]]** or for **[[16.11. Scroll Inscribing_|Scroll Inscribing]]**.

The **GM decides** whether the proposed research is possible or not. If so, PCs may engage in the research themselves (access to a library is usually required) or retain the services of an NPC spellcaster. Magical research to **learn a new spell** is generally possible as long as the PC has access to their spellbook.

#### Minor or Major Research

The GM decides whether the proposed research qualifies as **Minor** or **Major**, consistent with the setting. A **Magic User** researching a **spell** from the Magic chapter to add to their grimoire counts as Minor research. Researching a spell from an *[[27.5.3. Old Magic Grimoires|Old Magic Grimoire]]* (Treasure chapter) counts as Major research. Magic Users may also create their own unique spells; the GM determines whether the suggested spell counts as Minor or Major research. **Modifiers** to any checks are at the GM's discretion.
