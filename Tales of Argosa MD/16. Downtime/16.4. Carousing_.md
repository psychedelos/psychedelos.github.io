Carousing covers a range of activities, but primarily drinking, gambling, brawling, drug use, and related vices. Assuming a suitable tavern, inn, tap room, etc is available, a PC may carouse at any time. A minimum of spend of **20 sp** is required, triggering a **d100 roll**. Many of the entries below will benefit from further elaboration; players are encouraged to improvise these, subject to the GM's approval or at least rueful acquiescence. Where named NPCs are involved, reroll any repeats.

If carousing during **Downtime**, each **20 sp** spent earns the adventurer **1 xp,** up to a maximum per Downtime period as follows: **Level 1-3: 5 xp**, **Level 4-6: 10 xp**, or **Level 7-9: 20 xp.**
