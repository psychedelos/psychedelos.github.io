A character skilled in Animal Lore may attempt to train an ordinary or monstrous animal to follow directions and fight beside them. Such pets are treated like **hirelings**; they act of their own accord, apply the usual morale rules, and die at zero hp (at the GM's option, the PC may spend a point of ***Luck*** to allow the pet a **Death save**). Directing a pet to attack a target other than the one the PC is attacking, or to perform a specific trick under pressure, usually requires an action (and perhaps a Cha (Animal Lore) check; GM's call).

### Ordinary Pets

Training an ordinary pet to fight with the PC, or to perform specific tricks under pressure, requires **2d6 weeks,** 10 sp per week, and a **Cha (Animal Lore\*)** check. Roll **d66** for pet qualities; the GM decides the impact of traits if/when required.

### Monstrous Pets

Most monstrous animals (*[[Owlbear|Owlbears]], [[Griffon|Griffons]],* etc) are not capable of being trained; their instinctive ferocity and distrust of bipeds renders such attempts doomed to failure. Assuming the PC can find a suitable candidate however (an adventure in itself), training a monstrous pet requires **4d6 weeks** of intensive work, 50 sp per week, and a successful **Montage** (Moderate or Hard). A monster's combative nature imposes disadvantage on all training checks. If failed, the beast proves intractable.

Even if successfully "trained" however, monstrous pets sometimes revert to their primal urges, particularly when injured. Each time such a pet inflicts or suffers **critical** damage, or is **Wounded**, a **Will check** (by the beast) is required to avoid rolling **1d6** on the *Monstrous Instincts* table.
