Adventurers gamble with their lives, so why not their coin? Given the objective of ToA is for the party to go on exciting adventures in search of wealth and power, the scope for making coin via gambling is intentionally limited.

During Downtime, an adventurer may spend **one week** gambling, wagering up to **100 sp** during this time. At the end of this period, the gambler makes a ***Luck*** **save**. If the PC attempts to cheat or leverage some advantage, the GM makes a ruling and determines any modifiers.

If the *Luck* save is failed, the PC loses their coin. On a Terrible Failure, they lose double their wager. If they cannot pay, they are indebted to a local criminal, or made an example of (*[[13.2.3. Blunt Trauma|Blunt Trauma]]* (1d6+6, but no permanent injuries). If successful, the PC doubles their money, or on a Great Success, triples it. If they win, the PC begins their next adventure with their *Luck* reduced by 1 point.
