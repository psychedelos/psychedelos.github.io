Rare items **cost 5d10+50 sp** and are generally found in cities or wealthy towns.

> [!note]
> For **random** items **roll 1d20**: 1 Acid, 2-3 Antitoxin, 4-5 **Fire Pot**, 6-7 **Healer's Kit**, 8 Hook Hand, 9 Magnifying Glass, 10 Spyglass, 11 Peg Leg, 12 **Poison**, 13 Poppy Milk, 14 Poisoner's Ring, 15 **Thieves' Tools**, 16-17 Smelling Salts, 18 Tripwires, 19 **Trollblood Ointment**, 20 Yellow Lotus Powder.

### Acid

A vial of acid may (slowly) melt through locks, hinges, chains, etc. It may also be thrown (Close range) at an enemy, causing 1d8 acid damage.

### Antitoxin

Antitoxin applied within one minute of poisoning grants a second *Luck* save to resist the poison or some other beneficial effect at the GM's discretion. It does not undo attribute or hit point loss already suffered, but will stave off further loss, paralysis, or death. Antitoxin may also be taken ahead of time, granting the same protection for 2d6 rounds.

### Fire Pot

The fire pot is a thrown missile weapon (range Far, +2 bonus on attack roll) which explodes in a Melee area, causing 1d10 damage (Nat 19: *Luck* (Con) save or *[[12.11. Conditions_#Burning|Burning]]*) and creating a thick cloud of smoke (*[[12.11. Conditions_#Near Blind|Near Blind]]*, disperses after 1 round).

### Healer's Kit

A Healer's kit comes with two uses and contains bandages, balms, etc. It may assist with recovery during [[14.1. Short Rest_#Healer's Kit|Short Rests]].

### Hook Hand

For those absent a forearm, the practicalities of a hook hand cannot be understated. Hooks act as daggers if used as a weapon and may be attached to custom shields. Advanced replacements are not unknown, including life like shapes, tools or weapons, hidden compartments, etc.

### Magnifying Glass or Spyglass

A magnifying glass assists in locating tiny clues and starting fires (magnifying daylight). A spyglass telescope allows the user to see three times as far.

### Peg Leg

A lower leg prosthetic to replace lost limbs beneath the knee. With practice the subject will walk at normal speed. The user suffers disadvantage on movement based Dex checks, including *Luck* (Dex) saves. Advanced replacements are not unknown, similar to hook hands.

### Poison

Poisons come in a wide variety of effects as noted in the Traps section ([[26.4.18. Poison|p.226]]). Different poisons are administered in different ways, by skin contact, blood injury, inhalation, and/or ingestion (GM's call). Applying poison to a weapon takes an **action**, and remains until a strike is delivered or one minute passes (at which time the poison has either run off the blade or become inert after exposure to air). The GM decides which poisons (if any) are available for sale on a case by case basis.

### Poppy Milk

This cloudy white tincture is a morphine like pain killer that suspends or mitigates most persistent injury effects for two hours (GM's call). Only one effect may be suspended at a time, and the user is drained 1 point of Perc, Will and Int. Each use carries a 20% chance of Addiction ([[16.8. Recovery_#Addiction|p.124]]).

### Poisoner's Ring

This ring includes a small secret compartment to store one dose of poison or some other tiny object. Some variants include a pop out needle.

### Thieves' Tools

Skeleton keys, picks, files, saws, clamps and pliers, this tool kit allows a character to pick locks or disarm traps. Without such tools, picking locks or disarming traps may not be possible (GM's call).

### Smelling Salts

If a PC who survives their *[[13.1. Injury & Death_#Death Save|Death save]]* is exposed to smelling salts, they regain 1 hp in one round instead of 1d3 minutes. Comes with two doses.

### Tripwires

Commonly a spool of thin fishing line, strung between ankle height weights topped with bells or connected to a trap of some kind (such as a brace of rigged crossbows on a wooden frame).

### Trollblood Ointment

This non magical admixture may be applied to ease painful bruises, clot wounds, and scab over cuts with disturbing alacrity. The ointment works like *[[Mend Flesh|Mend Flesh]]*, but heals 1 hp per round for 5 rounds. It does not affect persistent injuries, and acid or fire ends the regeneration. A character may only benefit from *Trollblood Ointment* once per day.

### Yellow Lotus Powder

*Yellow Lotus Powder* produces a two hour euphoria and boosts social temperament (+2 bonus on social checks, and may spend a *Reroll* even if unskilled). Side effects include disinhibition, elevated heart rate, and in long time users yellowing eyeballs. Each use has a 20% chance of Addiction ([[16.8. Recovery_#Addiction|p.124]]).
