Lifestyle expenses amalgamate periodic costs for food, shelter, clothing, utilities, etc. Rich & Filthy Rich lifestyles might include servants, livestock, land, special functions, informants, and so on.

- **Street:** Homeless. Persistent injuries may not heal (GM's call). At least it's free.
- **Poor:** 10 sp/week. A bunk and table in a small, dark, cold room.
- **Middle:** 40 sp/week. Moderate sized room with a fire.
- **Rich:** 100 sp/week. Large rooms, warm and secure, fine furnishings.
- **Filthy Rich:** 300+ sp/week. The finest rooms, servants and guards, rare delicacies, etc.
