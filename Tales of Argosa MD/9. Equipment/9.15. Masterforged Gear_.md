On rare occasions, mastersmiths may be available to be commissioned to fashion bespoke weapons and armour of unmatched quality. Made of flawless or advanced materials, utilising superior designs, or perfectly weighted to match the owner's physiology, such armaments outperform similar equipment of their kind. Assuming a mastersmith can be retained (GM's call), adventurers may spend **2d6 x 100 sp** to order a masterforged weapon or shield, or **3d6 x 100 sp** for masterforged armour. Forging *silvered, cold iron, mithral* or *adamantine* versions add their usual additional cost (p.58). A weapon or armour may only have one masterforged quality. Creation times are typically **1d4+3 weeks** for a weapon or shield, and **1d6+4 months** for armour. The GM is encouraged to create their own custom masterforged qualities.
