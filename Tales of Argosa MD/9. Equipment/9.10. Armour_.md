A character's **Armour Class (AC) = 10 + armour AC bonus + Dex mod** (if any). Heavier armours impose penalties on certain actions, as indicated below. **Animal barding** costs five times the price of human armour.

For **random armour** roll **1d6**: 1-2 Light, 3 Medium, 4 Heavy, 5-6 Shield.

### Armour

| TYPE | SUP | TRAITS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light**<br>Leather, hide, robes, etc. | Com<br>5 sp | **+1 AC**<br>No penalties. |
| **Medium**<br>Chain shirt, breastplate, bone, etc. | Unc<br>40 sp | **+3 AC**<br>-2 penalty on stealth and swimming related checks. |
| **Heavy**<br>Plate mail, chain mail, ring mail, etc. | Rare<br>100 sp | **+5 AC**<br>As above plus -2 penalty on running and climbing related checks. Ignore negative Dex mod to AC. |
