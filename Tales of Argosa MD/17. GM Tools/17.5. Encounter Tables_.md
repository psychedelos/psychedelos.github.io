The *[[17.5.2. City, Town, Village|Cities, Towns, & Villages]]* table has fifty entries that are almost all non-combat encounters. The other tables have twenty encounters, divided up into approximately 70% "monster" encounters, 15% hazards, and 15% seeds for possible side treks. Monster encounters need not lead to combat if using *[[11.1. Reaction Roll_|Reaction]]* and *[[11.2. Activity Die_|Activity]]* rolls. To **swap one creature for another**, roll on the *[[21.2.1. Monsters by HD|Monster by HD]]* chart (may require some tweaking, eg a desert terrain *[[Slop Gorger|Slop Gorger]]* might be a *Sand Gorger*, etc).
