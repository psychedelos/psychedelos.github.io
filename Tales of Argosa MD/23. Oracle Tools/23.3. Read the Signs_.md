The **Deck of Signs** is an oracle tool to draw from when you have an **open question** that requires more than a yes or no answer.

The cards are in a similar style to the tarot's major arcana, made up of and depicting various fantasy archetypes and icons. Each card has **four words** related to its theme from which to draw inspiration: a **Noun** (the archetype name, top left), **Verb** (a doing word, top right), **Adjective** (a descriptive noun, for more nuanced detail, bottom left), and a general **Ambience/Tone/Mood Tag** for broader context (bottom right).

When you *Read the Signs*, pose an open question to be answered and **draw two cards,** then interpret the answer using two or more of the words and/or art presented. A single card also works, but drawing two cards enables more obvious and varied prompt combinations. Remember the deck is just another tool in your arsenal, to be used with your other favourite oracles, tables, etc.

> [!example]
> You *Read the Signs* to learn more about a random barbarian tribe in a ruin. Drawing two cards reveals **Magician, Conjuring, Learned, Enchanted** and **Death, Decaying, Uncaring, Doomed.** Applying Magician, Doomed, and Decaying, you interpret this to mean the Thuel tribe camping in the ruins have been cursed by a resident undead sorcerer!

### Time

Each card includes a **sun** or **moon** icon to generate random times, if required:

- **Light Sun = Morning** (6am to Noon)
- **Dark Sun** = **Afternoon** (Noon to 6pm)
- **Light Moon = Evening** (6pm to Midnight)
- **Dark Moon** = **Late Night** (Midnight to 6am)

If the particular hour is required, roll 1d6, starting from 6am, Noon, 6pm, or Midnight respectively.

### Direction

The cards also includes a small **compass symbol** with an arrow, indicating direction.
