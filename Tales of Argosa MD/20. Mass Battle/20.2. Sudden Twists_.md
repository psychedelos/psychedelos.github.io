In mass battle the PCs are in the thick of extreme danger; swept up in a gruesome throng of panicked shouts, slashing blades, and deadly arrows. Amidst the chaos, injury and death threaten from all corners! During a **Field Event**, if combat is occurring nearby, every time a PC rolls a **natural 1 or 20** on an attack roll or other 1d20 check, a **Sudden Twist** is triggered. **Roll 1d20** and see below.
