**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** Special

You ask a humanoid corpse or bones with a skull three questions, and it answers with knowledge the corpse had while alive. The pair of you converse in a sorcerous tongue no-one else understands. Some answers may be cryptic in nature (GM's call). Consorting with the dead corrupts the mind; when the spell ends *Consult the Bones (unlikely)* to determine whether you suffer a Madness (a Great Success negates).
