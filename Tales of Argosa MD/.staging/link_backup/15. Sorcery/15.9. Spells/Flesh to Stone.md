**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** Permanent until dispelled

This potent curse transforms flesh into stone or vice versa. A touch attack (at adv) is required, forcing the target to make a *Luck* (Con) save. On a success the petrification is negated but the target is *Dazed*. A character that saves cannot be retargeted by this spell in the same encounter. Petrified creatures are in stasis and do not age until the enchantment ends (eg by way of *Sever Arcarnum*, *Cleansing Charm,* or similar). On a Great Success the effect cannot be dispelled without Ritual Magic.
