**Range:** Touch  
**Duration:** Permanent

This accursed necromancy animates a humanoid corpse as a *Skeleton* or *Zombie*. As part of another action, you may issue spoken orders which the *Undead* obeys. The monster acts at the same time as you in future turns.

Animating the dead is fraught with peril and always triggers a *Dark & Dangerous Magic* effect. On a Great Success you may animate two corpses.
