**Range:** Close  
**Duration:** 2d6 hours

One object produces light, from a dim glow to a bright radiance that illuminates a Far sized area (action to change brightness). Alternatively you may cast this spell on a target's eyes to *Blind* it (*Luck* (Con) save negates). On a Great Success, you may produce sunlight, and if the object strikes a *Demon* or *Undead*, they suffer 1d6 extra damage.
