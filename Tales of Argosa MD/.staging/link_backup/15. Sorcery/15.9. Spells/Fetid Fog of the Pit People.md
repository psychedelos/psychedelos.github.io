**Range:** Far  
**Duration:** 1d6 x 10 mins

You conjure a Close sized area of thick, sickly coloured fog. The fog does not completely obscure sight but *Near Blind* applies. Creatures in the cloud must make a *Luck* (Con) save or lose their action and move due to nausea for 1d4 rounds, after which time they stagger out. On a Great Success, the save is made at a -2 penalty. This is a Poison effect.
