**Range:** Very Far  
**Duration:** Instant

Lightning blasts outwards from you in a straight line up to Very Far range. Targets in the path suffer 1d6 damage per caster level, on a Great Success roll damage with advantage. A *Luck* (Dex) save halves the damage, on a Terrible Failure also *Dazed*. Flammable objects in the area may catch fire.
