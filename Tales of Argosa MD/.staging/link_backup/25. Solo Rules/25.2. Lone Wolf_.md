For a solitary PC, some rules changes are required to give them a fighting chance in the wilds of Argosa. The Lone Wolf is a **hero among heroes,** emulating the likes of Conan, Elric, etc.

A **Lone Wolf PC** applies the following:

- Starts at **Level 3.**
- Hit Points equal to **25 + Class bonus.**
- When taking an action in combat (exc Dodge) they make a **bonus melee** or **ranged attack.**
- Each time the PC suffers a melee or ranged hit, roll the **Fortune die** (p.212). On a [sun icon] the attack is parried, evaded, or negated by some other means. On a [skull icon] the PC counterstrikes, automatically causing standard melee (or ranged, if more appropriate) damage to their opponent. On a **Nil** (blank) result there is no special effect.
- If reduced to **zero hit points**, unless the enemy ensures the PC is dead, the PC makes a *Death save* after the combat or encounter. If they live, they auto stabilize (they don't need anyone to aid them).
- On a **Short Rest**, make **three Willpower** checks instead of two. If the PC's *Luck* is less than 10, on a Great Success, the PC may restore 1 point of *Luck* as a further option.
- If the PC is facing **five or more** enemies, **halve** their number.
- During **Hexploration**, the PC always counts as being on **Look Out** duty (p.135), even when asleep (sixth sense for danger, light sleeper, etc).This is a bonus Travel Role; they still choose another role to perform each day.
