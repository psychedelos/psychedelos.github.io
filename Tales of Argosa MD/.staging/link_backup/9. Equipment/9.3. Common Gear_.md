Common equipment **costs 1d3 sp** and is available in most villages . Most examples below take up **one item slot** (worn items do not take up slots, and two handed items take two slots). Smaller quantities of different types of items might be bundled into a **misc bundle** slot (GM's call). Rations include water. Arrows typically use Ammo rolls\* p.58.
