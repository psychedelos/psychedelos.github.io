| 2-3 | **Long Arm of the Law** Word of the deal reaches the ears of the guard. Roll 1d6: (i-iii) Sergeant, (iv-v) Captain, or (vi) Commander of the watch, who takes a personal interest in the case. |
| 4 | **Scam** The other party attempts a scam. Roll 1d6: (i) Takes the deposit and wastes PC's time, then vanishes, (ii) Produces a counterfeit object or payment, (iii) Engages with the PC but endlessly delays (never runs out of excuses), (iv) Delivers something similar, but different, to what was promised, (v) Demands more gold (or a discount if buying) at the final meet, (vi) Engages with the PC but is killed by rivals before the final meet. |
| 5 | **Impostor** The other party is not who they profess to be. Roll 1d6: (i) Undercover guard, (ii) Rival faction spy, (iii) Assassin paid to kill the PC for a past wrong, (iv) Thug planning to rob the PC, (v) Slaver about to spring their trap, (vi) Vigilante who intends to expose the PC for the base ne'er do well they really are. |
| 6-8 | **All Clear** No complications this time around. As far as the adventurer can tell. |
| 9 | **Bounty Hunters** Third party agents get wind of the transaction and take steps to arrest the PC for a bounty, to expunge a debt, acquire the item for themselves, etc. 1d2+1 bounty hunters, random classes, level 1d4+2 (use Rival Adventurers). |
| 10 | **Bidding War** Word of the transaction is leaked to other interested parties, who start a bidding war for the item. Increase the price by 1d100% + 50%. Nothing personal. Just business. |
| 11-12 | **Doublecross** The other party attempts to doublecross the PC. Roll 1d6: (i) Ambush with intent to kill the PC, (ii) Ambush with intent to rob the PC, (iii) Reports the PC to the guard (secret informant), (iv) Completes the transaction, but later attempts to blackmail the PC, (v) Reports the transaction to a known enemy of the PC, (vi) While the adventurer is meeting about the deal, the other party arranges for the PC's home/room to be burgled. |
