Magic Users (and at the GM's option Cultists) may create scrolls during Downtime. The inscriber requires access to a spellbook/scriptures, as well as a writing surface and ink.

Every scroll requires a **special material** that the PC must supply (generally rare hides or fluids, which spoil or otherwise become unsuitable after 2d6 months). In most instances the material must somehow be consistent with the spell to be scribed. The GM decides whether the proposed material matches the spell, guided by the table opposite.

The inscribing process **costs (1d4+2) x 100 sp** and takes **2d6 days** of ritual etching. Any spell from the Spell List (p.100) may be inscribed, or other spell the Magic User knows or is contained in a spellbook they have access to.

An **Int (Arcane Lore\*)** check is required at the end of the process. On a **success**, the scroll is made and functions at the creator's level, applying success or Great Success to the spell effect as per the Int (Arcane Lore\*) check. The creator of the scroll is **drained 1 Con,** and lost Con cannot be restored until the end of the next Downtime period after the scroll is used. On a **failure** the scroll fails and any materials are lost (or, GM's call, the scroll is made but when used auto triggers a DDM effect). On a Terrible Failure, a DDM effect is also triggered.

### Scroll Materials

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Elemental effects** | Monsters with similar abilities. Eg *Frost Giant* blood for *Blast of Frozen Ruin* or *Hell Hound* hide for *Hellblast*. |
| **Healing** | Monsters with regen or classic good or holy creatures. Eg *Troll* blood for *Mend Flesh* or *Unicorn* tears for *Cleansing Charm.* |
| **Curses or "evil" effects** | Classic evil or unholy monsters. Eg *Demon* ichor for *Thrice Bound Curse* or bottled *Spectre* essence for *Undead Servant.* |
| **Illusion** | Monsters with similar abilities. Eg *Invisible Stalker* saliva for *A Wisp Unseen* or *Imp* marrow for *Shadows & Dust.* |
| **Protection** | Monsters with similar or opposite abilities, or perhaps classic good or or resilient creatures. For example *Stone Golem* dust for *Arcane Aegis* or *Water Elemental* fluid for *Elemental Ward.* |
| **Conjuration** | Monsters of a similar kind or inherently magical creatures. Eg *Gibbering Terror* tentacle juice for *Conjure Entity*. |
| **Transmutation** | Amorphous monsters or creatures with similar abilities. Eg *Basilisk* bile for *Flesh to Stone* or *Grey Ooze* sludge for *Transmogrification*. |
| **Mental effects** | Monsters with psychic, charm, fear, etc effects. Eg *Harpy* milk for *Gaze of Beguilement.* |
| **Divination** | Monsters with augury powers or creatures strongly connected to the Veil. For example *Hag* blood for *Sight Beyond Sight* or *Ghoul* teeth for *Riddle of Bones.* |
