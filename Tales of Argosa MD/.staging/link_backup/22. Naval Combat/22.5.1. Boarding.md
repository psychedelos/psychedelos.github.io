| 1 | **KO!** Flying debris during an artillery salvo knocks the PC out for 2d6 rounds (*Luck* (Con) save resists). |
| 2 | **Rolling Deck** A big wave, artillery or other effect rocks the ship violently. All PCs must make a Dex check or fall *Prone* (2 out of 3 crew fall prone). On a TF, PC is *Stunned*. |
| 3 | **Explosion!** A cannonball or other artillery causes an explosion of timber, cargo, etc in the PC's area: 2d6 damage and roll *Blast Trauma* (1d12). *Luck* (Con) save negates the trauma roll. |
| 4 | **Pile On!** 1d4+1 enemies suddenly appear through a door or hallway, and pile onto the PC, pinning them down (Str contest vs Str 16 to resist being *Helpless*). |
| 5 | **Unexpected Aid** At the right moment, an allied crew member, escaped prisoner, or stowaway appears from behind cargo, drops from the rafters, etc, taking out 1d4 of the PC's opponents with an improvised weapon (heavy crate, skillet, oar, etc). |
| 6 | **Pistol Shot** rings out as a nearby enemy attempts to snipe the PC from a doorway, popping out from behind cover, etc (make an attack roll at +2, pistol damage). |
| 7 | **Grenade!** Sounds of nearby battle can be heard as a fizzing powder grenade bounces or skitters into the area the PC is fighting in. It will explode in 1d2 rounds! |
| 8 | **Fire!** A shattered lantern sets part of the ship on fire. Unless doused by the end of the round, apply Artillery #19. |
| 9 | **Blocked** The closest hallway or stairs is suddenly blocked by broken timber, smoke, fire, shifting cargo, etc. |
| 10 | **When Animals Attack** A feline, monkey, parrot, ferret, or other ship mascot drops from the rafters, claws outstretched, latching onto the PC's head (*Blind*, spend an action to make a Str check to remove). |
| 11 | **Barrage!** A volley of arrows, bolts, or shots rain down on the PC and any characters within Melee range of her (3d6 damage, *Luck* (Dex) save for half). |
| 12 | **Entangled** PC's foot becomes entangled in coils of spare rigging/rope, threatening to trip them over (Dex check avoids). |
| 13 | **Duck!** A bulky iron rigging hook swings down from a broken line. The PC must make a Dex check or suffer 1d8+2 damage and be *Stunned*. |
| 14 | **Lost Gear** While boarding or skirmishing, the PC loses a random piece of Battle Gear. *Consult the Bones* to determine if the item falls overboard. |
| 15 | **Blast!** An artillery explosion on deck threatens to blast the PC (and foes in melee) overboard, *Luck* (Dex) save resists. |
| 16 | **Next** An enemy crew veteran (Fighter 3+) calmly skewers her current foe, then makes eye contact with the PC, and drags her thumb across her tattooed neck. |
| 17 | **Net** An enemy drops a weighed net from the rigging above, entangling the PC (action and Str contest vs 16 to escape, otherwise *Helpless*). |
| 18 | **Poison** The PC's opponent is using a rare *Sea Serpent* poison. If already injured, the poison kicks in now (otherwise next hit): 4d6 damage and *Luck* (Con) save or random limb paralysed for 1d4 days. |
| 19 | **Sniper!** A sniper in the crow's nest takes a pot shot at the PC (+3 to hit, crossbow or musket damage). |
| 20 | **The hell is that!?** A colossal tentacle (Str 22) rises from nearby water, snatching a random character (PC or NPC) off the deck, dragging them beneath the water (PCs get a *Luck* (Dex) save to negate). This happens each round until the beast has had its fill (*Consult the Bones*, or GM's call) |
