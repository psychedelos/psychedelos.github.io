In the spirit of quick and emergent play, **player character** creation is mostly **randomised by default**. For tables that prefer more control, modify as desired (the **Variant Rules** chapter p.260 has some suggestions).

1. **Roll 1d12 for Race:** 1-9 Human, 10 Dwarf, 11 Elf, 12 Halfling or Half Skorn (p.13).
2. **Roll d66 for Background.** Each entry grants an attribute bonus (max 16), a skill, and an item.
3. **Generate Attributes** by rolling 3d6 seven times and allocate the results in order to Str, Dex, Con, Int, Perc, Will, and Cha. You must have at least one attribute of **13 or higher** and another of **15 or higher**. If not, increase one attribute of your choosing to 13, and/or one to 15, as required. You may then also **swap any two** Attribute scores, if desired.
4. **Choose your Class:** Artificer, Bard, Fighter, Barbarian, Monk, Ranger, Rogue, Cultist, or Magic User.
5. **Your Luck** is 11, **Rerolls** are 2 (Human) or 1 (other races), and **DDM** 1.
6. **Initiative** is the average of Dex and Int (round down as noted on p.7).
7. **Hit Points** are calculated according to your Class (Con score +1, 2 or 3 points).
8. **Your Death save** is 10 + your Con mod or Will mod, whichever is higher (min 10).
9. **Skills** are partly based on your class, and partly rolled. If you get the same skill twice, roll again.
10. **Class Abilities** for 1st Level may be set or rolled for, depending on your class.
11. **Battle Gear** slots are half your Str (round down, min 5), and the other half are **Pack Gear** (round up, min 5, p.55). Add a torch, bedroll, rations (5 days), and tinderbox to Pack Gear. Add 10 sp to Coins. Then **add Class Starting Gear.**
12. **Your Armour Class (AC)** equals 10 + your Armour bonus (+1 if shield) + Dex mod.
13. **Your Attack Bonus** equals your Class Attack Bonus + Str modifier (for melee) or Perc modifier (for ranged).
14. **Choose bonus Languages** equal to your Int mod (if -2 or -3, you are illiterate).
15. **Your Age is 1d20+16 years** (young - middle aged) or humans may choose to be **old (1d10+60).** Old PCs reduce their Str & Con by one, increase their Int & Will by one (max 16), and begin an adventure with one extra use of their 1st Level Class Ability.
16. **Name** your Adventurer. The Hireling tables (p.156) offer example **Names, Personalities,** and a physical **Trait** if desired (ignore entries 23-24 and 91-92, and if any injuries the PC has already adapted to them p.124).
17. **Finally,** roll **d66** for a **Party Bond** (unless you already have one) p.44.
