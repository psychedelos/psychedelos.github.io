From time to time the GM may rule that a simple random check, eg a **d100 percentage, x-in-6, or** ***Consult the Bones*** **roll** (p.212) will resolve the uncertainty of a situation. For example, with a *Basilisk* surrounded by three PCs, the GM decides the beast will attack *Kruncor* on a d6 roll of 1-2, *Lyssa* on a 3-4, or their poor hireling *Ratsack* on a 5-6. Additionally, certain powerful monsters may have percentile based abilities, such as *Magic Resistance.*
