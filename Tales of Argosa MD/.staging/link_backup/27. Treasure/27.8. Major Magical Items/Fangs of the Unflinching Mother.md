This *silvered* shortsword has a snake head pommel encrusted with emerald eyes. Forged by the serpentmen of the Second Age, the blade was empowered by the snake goddess and the *Nest Beyond the Stars*. Like the Scaled Ones themselves, there have been no reports of the blade in the current era.

### Unflinching Fangs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Spend a *Reroll* (no action) to clone the shortsword into two weapons. The cloned weapon lasts 2d6 rounds, and may be given to another if desired. If Two Weapon Fighting with both shortswords, the user does not suffer disadvantage on their Extra attack. Only one cloned weapon may exist at a time. |
| **2nd** | Once every 1d4 days, coat one or both shortswords in a random poison (roll 1d20 on the Poison table). The venom lasts until the next successful strike or 2d6 rounds, whichever occurs first. |
| **3rd** | Once per week, seize control of a reptilian creature (as *Sign of Subjugation,* 8th level, and the target need not be humanoid). |
| **4th** | Once every six months, transform into a *Giant Serpent* (as *Transmogrification* at 8th level, but 12 HD, Bite 2d8 + Poison, and the effect lasts 2d6 minutes). |
| **Special** | Your canines and tongue enlarge; not enough to notice with a casual glance, but close inspection reveals the abnormality (Perc check, perhaps opposed by a Cha (Deception or Sleight of Hand) roll). If the sword is discarded, these effects revert over 1d12 months. |
