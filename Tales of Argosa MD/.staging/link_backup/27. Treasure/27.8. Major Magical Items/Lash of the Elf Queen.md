This slender whip is cut from wyvern hide, decorated with tiny barbs, with an orange topaz set into the haft. The lash belonged to the elf queen *Ninsorva*, renowned for her enslavement of all sapient races within the boughs of *Cemorril* (now called the *Wistwood*) during the dawn of the Second Age. The whip was lost with the fall of the last elven kingdom.

### Lash of the Elf Queen

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once per week, you may cause the lash to animate and act on its own (as *Infuse Animus* at 7th level, affects the lash only). |
| **2nd** | On a critical hit, the whip tears off one of the target's limbs (no save, targets with 7+ HD are tripped or disarmed instead). |
| **3rd** | Once per month, by wrapping the whip around your waist, you fade from sight and hearing (as *A Wisp Unseen* and *Silent Shroud* at 7th level; only you are affected). |
| **Special** | The lash causes 2d4 damage on a hit instead of 1d4. You develop a bond with nature and a haughty, more ambitious streak, less content with your place in the world (treat as elf personality traits, p.14). If you discard the lash, this effect dwindles over 1d12 months. |
