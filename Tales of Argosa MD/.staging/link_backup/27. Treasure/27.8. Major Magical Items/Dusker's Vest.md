This black leather cuirass was cut from the hide of an elder cloaker, marked with a moon sigil and sewn with numerous secret pockets. It may be worn as light armour or as part of a set of medium armour. Used for centuries by assassins of the enigmatic *Duskers*, the armour entered general circulation after a botched murder in *Crow's Keep.*

### Dusker's Vest

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once every 1d4 days, draw a useful item from one of the vest's secret pockets (treat as UF *Pilfer Pouch*). |
| **2nd** | Once per week, you invoke a chameleon effect, gaining adv on stealth checks, and allowing you to hide in plain sight. The effect lasts 2d6 minutes. |
| **3rd** | Once per month, you may merge your physical body with your shadow (no action). Whist in two dimensional shadow form, you must move along surfaces, but may slip beneath doors, between cracks, etc. Further, you may merge indistinguishably into other larger shadows, and are immune to non-magical weapons (excluding *cold iron*). The effect lasts up to 2d6 minutes. |
| **Special** | You find yourself inclined to profitable criminal activities such as robbery, black market fencing, and fraud. At the GM's option, resisting such opportunities might require a Will check from time to time (treat as a Madness). If you discard the vest, this effect fades over 1d6 months. |
