Created by the *Frost Giants* of *Varn Karagoss,* this enchanted horn was crafted from a mammoth's tusk. The horn is burnished to a pearly grey, gold clasps inlaid with tracings of blue topaz, and intrinsically alters size to match the user. Binding the merciless cold and biting winds of the *White Drifts*, the horn is no instrument for old men.

### Horn of Karagoss

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once per week, sound the horn to lash foes with a hail of deadly frost (as *Blast of Frozen Ruin* at 8th Level). Also immune to cold damage (magical or otherwise). |
| **2nd** | Once per month, sound the horn to summon a 10 HD *Snow Elemental* (as *Water Elemental*). It obeys your orders (no action) and lasts 2d6 rounds. |
| **3rd** | Once every six months, sound the horn for over the course of 1 hour to summon fog, torrential rain, a blizzard, or similar cold, horrible weather (as *Conclave of Wind & Sky*, at 9th level). |
| **Special** | You grow four inches taller, develop an aversion to warmth (feels uncomfortable), and a silver streak in your hair. Using any of the horn's powers requires a Con check. If unsuccessful, the horn fails to function and saps your strength (lose 1d3 Str, Con or Dex; determine randomly). You may try again next turn, if you wish. |
