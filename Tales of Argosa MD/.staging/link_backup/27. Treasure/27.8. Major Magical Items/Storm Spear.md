This hardwood spear has a twin bladed bronze head, with lightning like verdigris and runes along the shaft.

The creator is uncertain, but the forge work ancient in nature, and appears to date from the Second Age. Scattered reports from the *Ordo Malefactos* suggest the relic might be lost somewhere in the *Trackless Moors.*

### Storm Spear

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | Once every 1d4 days, slam the butt of the spear into the ground, causing a wave of thunder to boom outward (as *Thunderous Invocation* at 6th level). |
| **2nd** | Once per week, throw the spear to transform it into a bolt of lightning (as *Lightning Bolt* at 10th level, Great Success). The spear reappears in your hand at the end of the round. |
| **3rd** | Once per month, invoke the sky spirits who bear you aloft on powerful winds (as *Wings of the Raven King* at 6th level). |
| **Special** | You feel a strong affinity for wind, rain, and storms. When the GM rolls for weather, shift the result one level towards inclement conditions. |
