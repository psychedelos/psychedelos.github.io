This fleecy brown cloak has grey furred shoulders, a deep cowl, and a metal clasp with a kraken icon. Carried to Argosa across the *Boreal Sea,* the Cowl was a gift from *Yirig the Black* to *Drar Norrvak.* It was lost years later amid the *Siltwater Isles*, when the raider's vessel was sunk by a pair of Argosan warships.

### Cowl of the North

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1st** | While wearing the cloak, you are immune to cold damage (magical or otherwise), and once per month, may transform into heavy mist (no action, as *Cradle of Formlessness* at 8th level). |
| **2nd** | While wearing the cloak, once per week, you may transform into a white furred wolf or owl (as *Transmogrification* at 8th level, but lasts up to 2d6 hours). |
| **3rd** | Once every year, you may invoke a weather effect similar to *Conclave of Wind & Sky* at 9th level. You may treat the casting as a Great Success, is desired. |
| **Special** | You slowly but surely convert to worship of the *Deep One*, the slumbering Varnori god of the inky depths. |
