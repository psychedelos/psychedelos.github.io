*Minor Magical Items* includes all manner of potions, scrolls, spellbooks, and minor permanent magical items.

Lore wise *Old Magic Grimoires* don't really fall into the category of "minor" but they have nevertheless been wrapped into in the Spellbooks category.

### Potions

Potions come in a variety of **containers**, roll **1d6:** (i) jug, (ii) vial, (iii) flask, (iv) waterskin, (v) powder satchels, (vi) bottle. The owner may **sip a potion** to try to obtain a clue about what it might do prior to imbibing it whole (GM's call). **Roll 1d100** to determine which potion. They last for **1d6 x 10 minutes** unless indicated otherwise, or the imbiber may end the effect early by spending an action. Spell effects are usually treated as an **ordinary success at 5th level** (GM's call). Each time a potion is fully imbibed, the subject must check for a *Dark & Dangerous Magic* effect (p.92). If an effect does not trigger, the imbiber's DDM tally increases by 1, as normal. Using more than one potion at a time causes *Toxin Trauma* (1d12).

### Scrolls

Magical scrolls contain spells that anyone may attempt to cast (including the illiterate). Part of their enchantment includes the ability to read and comprehend the arcane runes contained within. **Roll 1d100** and consult the Spell list to determine which spell the scroll holds.

**Magic Users** (and perhaps **Cultists** in some cases; GM's call) may use a scroll to cast the inscribed spell without making any checks. A **Non Magic User** attempting to cast a spell scroll requires the usual spell casting check. If failed however the spell is miscast, triggering a DDM effect (p.92). Spell effects are usually treated as a **Great Success at 7th level** (GM's call). Using a scroll to cast its spell destroys the scroll.

### Spellbooks

A Magic User requires spellbook(s) to protect and refresh their mind's capacity to work magic, and to research spells. During their adventures, PC Magic Users that acquire new spellbooks, scrolls, and so on may study them to learn new spells more easily. **Learning a new spell** from a spellbook or scroll is a Downtime activity (Magical Research, p.125).

If the PC has acquired an **Old Magic Grimoire,** roll **1d12** and consult the chart below to determine which spell it contains. Old Magic Grimoires may be cast like Scrolls, but at **10th level.**
