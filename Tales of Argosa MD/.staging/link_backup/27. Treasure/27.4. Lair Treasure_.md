*Lair treasure* is generally allocated according to the predominant or highest HD of the vanquished. An entry covers the spoils of several score to hundreds of monsters, consolidated in a base of operations such as a castle, cave complex, temple, etc.

In some instances however *Lair Treasure* might reflect the collection of an individual hoarder such as a *Dragon, Medusa,* or elderly sorcerer. The entries indicate how many items of each category are found, or the percentage chance for a single item.
