Hirelings are commonly from the lowest rungs of society, emboldening them in their quest for coin. The PC **Backgrounds** table (p.8) serves equally well for **non combatants**. For **mercenaries roll 1d8** (i) ex-city watch, (ii) ex-militia, (iii) bodyguard, (iv) pit fighter, (v) ex-soldier, (vi) thug (vii) ex-pirate (viii) green recruit.
