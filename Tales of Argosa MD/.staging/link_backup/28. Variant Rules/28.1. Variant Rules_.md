Outlined below are some possible rule variations to better tailor ToA to your preferences.

### Customised PC Creation

For those tables that prefer more customised, less random character creation, consider any or all of the following tweaks:

- Roll 3d6 or 4d6 (drop the lowest), and allocate as the player wishes. If using the 4d6 variant, PCs do not gain +1 attribute each level.
- Players choose their PC's skills.
- Players choose their PC's starting abilities.
- PCs gain a Unique Feature of their choice at 1st level (characters will be significantly more powerful out the gate).

### Swapping Out Class Abilities

When a PC gains a level, they may also swap out one known class ability for another. A Magic User wishing to swap a known spell for another must go through the usual Magical Research process.

### More Deadly

For a deadlier game, do not permit *Rerolls* on *Death saves*, and if a PC suffers more damage than half their max hp in one hit, they are automatically reduced to zero hp ("massive damage" rule).

### Less Deadly

For a less deadly game, consider some of the following options:

- Remove the limit of one *Reroll* per roll.
- Allow *Rerolls* to apply to any failed rolls made by the adventurers.
- Remove the one Rescue per adventure limit.
- Adventurers gain an extra 1 or 2 hp when leveling up. Note the more hp the party has, the more monsters are required to genuinely threaten them, which will extend fight times.
- Light armour grants 1 point of damage reduction, or 2 points for medium or heavy armour (perhaps with equal penalties to the character's Initiative attribute).

### Less Dark Magic

For less dark but no less dangerous magic mishaps, download the **Perilous Magic** table from our website to substitute for the DDM table.

### Less Dangerous Magic

For more predictable, less dangerous magic, use 1d20 for all DDM checks, not just for magic items.

### Individual Initiative

All PCs roll initiative each round, and take their turns before or after the monsters based on their personal initiative result.

### Simultaneous Initiative

All PC turns occur simultaneously with their respective opponent. If there are more enemies than PCs, any remaining enemies act after the PCs.

### Distances in Feet

For distances in feet rather than range bands, use the following:

- **Melee** range: **5 ft**
- **Close** range: **30 ft**
- **Far** range: **60 ft**
- **Very Far** range: GM's call up to **240 ft**

### Small Party Play

For the most part ToA and the *Argosa Sandbox* were playtested with parties of 3-4 PCs. If playing with a pair of characters, or just a single PC, consider using the Solo rules p.216.

### Str for Thrown Weapon Damage

Thrown weapon damage adds either Str or Perc modifier.

### Advancement beyond 9th level

Each level beyond 9th, PCs gain:

- 1 hp per level, and +1 attack bonus every two levels.
- New skill at 11th level,
- +1 AC at 12th level.
- Unique Feature at 13th level.

### Class, Race & Background as Skills

If individual skills are not your style, ignore the skill list and expand the Backgrounds rule to include Class and Race. If an action is closely connected to a character's Class, Race, or Background, they gain a +1 bonus on any required attribute check, and may use a *Reroll* if desired. The GM adjudicates such on a case by case basis.

### Longer Adventures

For lengthy adventures, apply the Lone Wolf *Luck* recovery rule p.216 to all PCs, allowing the party to prop their *Luck* back up when they drop below 10.
