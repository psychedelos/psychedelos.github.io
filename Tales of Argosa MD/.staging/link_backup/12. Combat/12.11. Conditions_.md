The following rules address common conditions characters may be subject to. Specific circumstances may **modify the default conditions** (for example some Monster entries) or per GM's call.

### Blind

A *Blind* combatant suffers a **66% miss chance** on direct attacks, assuming they can narrow down the target's location by some means. If the target cannot be located, no direct attack may be made. Area attacks may be unaffected at the GM's discretion. Opponents that can see clearly gain **advantage** on attacks against *Blind* targets.

### Burning

A *Burning* character suffers **1d6 damage** at the **start** of their turn. While burning, the character must make a **Will check** to do anything other than spend their action to put out the flames (automatic unless GM decides otherwise). If rolling on the *Injuries & Setbacks* table, *Cosmetic Scarring* become *Serious Scarring* instead.

### Dazed

A *Dazed* character's **next action** occurs **last** in the initiative order. In addition, until their next action, they **do not** make a free attack when an opponent fumbles or withdraws.

### Encumbered

An *Encumbered* (p.55) PC suffers **disad** on tests relating to Athletics, Acrobatics, Chases, and Party Retreats. They **cannot** charge or attempt Rescues, and Hexploration speed may be affected (p.134).

### Fatigued

A character loses **1 point of Con** each time they become *Fatigued*. In addition, they are treated as ***Encumbered*** until they get six hours sleep. Lost Con may be restored in the usual way. Characters that **do not sleep** suffer disad on all tests the next day, until they get at least six hours sleep.

### Grabbed

A *Grabbed* character suffers -2 on attack rolls and **cannot move** from their **location** without breaking the grab (**opposed Str check**, no action). Enemies gain a +2 attack bonus against them.

### Helpless

A *Helpless* target is completely unable to defend themselves and **may be killed** with a single melee action (or perhaps ranged action, GM's call). Typical examples include sleeping, unconscious, or trussed up head to toe with rope.

### Near Blind

Suffer a **33% miss chance** on all attacks.

### Prone

A *Prone* character suffers a **-2 penalty** on **melee** attack rolls, and melee **opponents** gain a **+2 bonus.** **Standing up** from *Prone* costs a character's Move, unless they make a *Luck* (Dex) save, or other check as the GM determines (eg Dex (Acrobatics\*)).

### Starving

A starving or dehydrated character loses **1 Str and Con per day** until death if either stat reaches zero. Eating or drinking removes the condition. Lost Str or Con may be restored in the usual way.

### Stunned

A *Stunned* character **loses their next action.**

### Wounded

A character at **half hit points** or below is *Wounded.*
