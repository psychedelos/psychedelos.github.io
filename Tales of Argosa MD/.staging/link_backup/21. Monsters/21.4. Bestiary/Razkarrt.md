*Razkarrt* are the noble caste of the serpentmen, ruling over their civilization with great cunning and ruthlessness. They seek to restore the serpentfolk to greatness, or in the alternative hasten mankind to final destruction. The *Razkarrt* often masquerade as humans, seeking positions of power with access to networks of spies and informants, reveling in manipulating the foolish warmbloods as their ultimate plans unfold.
