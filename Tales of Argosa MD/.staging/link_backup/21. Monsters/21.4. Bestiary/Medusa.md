A *Medusa* is a cursed humanoid with a female upper body and face, serpentine lower half and tail, and venomous snakes instead of hair. A mortal looking into a *Medusa's* eyes risks being turned to stone (reversible with a rare salve of *Razkarrt* bile, or the *Flesh to Stone* spell). Unless surprised, adventurers may choose to avert their gaze from the Medusa, effectively fighting the monster *Blind*.
