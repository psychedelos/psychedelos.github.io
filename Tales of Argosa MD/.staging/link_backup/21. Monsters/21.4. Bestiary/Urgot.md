*Urgot* are abominations of dark sorcery or the cursed bloodlines of humans whose ancestors made pacts with infernal powers. They differ from tribe to tribe but all display major mutations such as tentacles, shark teeth, deformed bodies and limbs, quadruped motion, etc. Roll on the *Mutations Table* for a special ability if desired. *Urgot* are intelligent, cruel and depraved pack hunters, often employing ritual scarring to mark seniority. They are drawn to magic and prefer to lair in the shadows of ancient temples, forests, and isolated mountains.

**Urgot Champions** have HD 4 and cause 2d8 damage with a bite, tentacle crush, etc. Some tribes include a shaman known as an **Urgozer,** with Int 16, HD 3+3, and **Spells (4)** *Dark Slumber, Crush of the Warp, Shadows & Dust, Brain Burst, Hellblast, Riddle of Bones.*
