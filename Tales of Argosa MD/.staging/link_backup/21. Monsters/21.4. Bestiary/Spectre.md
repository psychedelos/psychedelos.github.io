*Spectres* are *Incorporeal Undead* that pass through solid objects like water. They possess a malign intelligence and recall much of their former life, tainted by all consuming hate and torment. A *Spectre's* icy touch drains memories and life essence.

**SPECTRE** #1 **AC** 15 **HD** 7 **Touch** 1d10 + Drain 1 level until the end of the next Downtime period **Nat 19** Special **S**- **D**15 **C**- **I**13 **P**15 **W**15 **Ch**9 **L**9, **Fly** Fast. *Incorporeal Undead*. Immune to non magical weapons (excluding *cold iron*, holy water). On a Nat 19 the target and *Spectre* vanish to a nightmare realm of dark sorcery to finish their duel. If the *Spectre* is defeated, the target reappears. A PC drained to zero level dies and rises as a *Wraith* in 1d4 nights. **Reac** 2-8 Murderous 9-10 Malevolent 11-12 Infatuated.
