*Basartu*, also known as Veil Bound, are living humanoids possessed by malignant, otherworldly entities. They may adopt two forms: the ordinary body of their host, or a corrupted variant including horns, claws, glowing eyes, etc, and a tendency to levitate rather than walk. Killing a *Basartu's* vessel forces the daemon into an invisible, *Incorporeal* form that invades the body of the next closest sentient being that fails a *Luck* (Will) save. The only way to destroy the demon is through an exorcism, the details of which are determined by the GM.
