*Goblins* are 4 ft, spindly humanoids with prickly skin, pointed ears, and oversized jaws brimming with man eating teeth. They avoid daytime activity and inhabit dark woods and caverns, as sunlight is painful to their eyes (*Near Blind*). *Hobgoblins* are highly rare and reclusive goblin sorcerers with Int 16, HD 3+3, and **Spells (3)** *Dark Slumber, Crush of the Warp, None Shall Pass, Shadows & Dust.*
