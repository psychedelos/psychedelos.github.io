*Plague Worms* are 8 ft chitinous worms that burrow through rock and soil, their skin coated in a slick, disease laden fungus that imparts *Greyscale Blight* (p.131). Their diet consists of smaller annelids and other creatures they happen upon. In times of scarcity, the worms fall into a dormant state, subsisting on tiny organisms in the earth. *Plague Worms* are nomadic but will linger near places of food, such as underground cities.
