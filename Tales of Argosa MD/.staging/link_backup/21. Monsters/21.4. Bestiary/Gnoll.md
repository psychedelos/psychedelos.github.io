*Gnolls* are 7 ft, hyena like humanoids, with teeth filled snouts, rending claws, and furred torsos. They prefer light armour and ranged weapons to take advantage of their speed and natural skirmisher tendencies. Gnolls are strict carnivores and relish eating intelligent humanoids, whom they torture and sacrifice to their demon god. Gorging on the carrion of a sacrificial victim is one of the pack's highest honours. *Shamans* have Int 15, HD 4+2 and **Spells (3)** *Hunger for Blood, Fetid Fog of the Pit People, Bestial Communion, Glimpse the True Gods.*
