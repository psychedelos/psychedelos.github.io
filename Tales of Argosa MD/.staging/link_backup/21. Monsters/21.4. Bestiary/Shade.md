*Shades* are *Incorporeal Undead* manifesting as ink black shadows, existing only to steal the vitality from the living to sustain their cursed half life. *Elder Shades* are ancient versions with HD 7, drain 1d6 Str per hit, and control ordinary shades. Some also demonstrate spell like abilities similar to *Shadows & Dust, Place of Perfect Night, Sever Arcarnum* and *Shadowbolt* (as *Lightning Bolt*, but made of life leeching darkness rather than lightning).

**SHADE** #2d6 **AC** 12 **HD** 4 **Touch** 1d8 + 1d2 Str loss **Nat 19** *Luck* save or weapon or armour is turned to shadow **S**- **D**16 **C**- **I**10 **P**13 **W**10 **Ch**7 **L**7. **Move** Fast. *Incorporeal Undead.* Immune to non magical weapons (excluding *cold iron*, holy water). A victim reduced to zero Str is instantly slain, and rises as a *Shade* next midnight. **Reac** 2-12 Hostile.
