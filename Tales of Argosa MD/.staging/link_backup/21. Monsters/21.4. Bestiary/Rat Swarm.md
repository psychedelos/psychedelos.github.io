A rat swarm consists of hundreds of desperate, starving vermin. *Swarm* rules apply. Could also cover **Bat Swarms** (add Echolocation, Flying).

**RAT SWARM** #1 **AC** 11 **HD** 2 **Bites** 1d6 + disease **Nat 19** Overwhelmed! *Luck* (Str/Dex) save or *Helpless* for 1d3 rounds **S**3 **D**15 **C**4 **I**2 **P**10 **W**7 **Ch**4 **L**5. **Swarm** (p.170). Threatening the rats with fire may keep them at bay for a short time (Will check resists). After combat, anyone injured must make a *Luck* (Con) save or suffer a disease. **Reac** 2-3 Starving Rat Swarm! 4-5 Hostile 6-8 Threatening 9-10 Wary 11-12 Fearful.
