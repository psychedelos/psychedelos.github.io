Guardsmen, soldiers, mercenaries, and similar warriors are covered by this entry. Typically bearing chain armour, a shield, and a mace, sword, or spear. *Heavy Infantry* have AC 15 or 16 (with a shield). *Veterans* have HD 2. *Captains* have HD 3, superior weapons and armour, and might have a **Heavy** ability or class levels.
