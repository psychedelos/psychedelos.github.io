The *Purple Worm* is a colossal, 100 foot column of rubbery flesh, and a circular maw filled with row upon row of man sized teeth. They hunt in seas and burrow beneath the earth, rarely surfacing except in times of extreme scarcity, or summoned by apocalyptic cults. *Purple Worms* detect their prey through ground vibrations and echolocation.
