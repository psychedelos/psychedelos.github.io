*Hags* present as elderly, hideous crones. They roam or lair on the outskirts of human settlements, often posing as isolated hermits. They are highly intelligent monsters and often adopt the language and culture of the region. *Hags* feed on humans, preferably children and babes, but can subsist on animals if required. They possess preternatural strength and their fingernails lengthen to wolf like claws when fighting.

A *Hag* may enchant the eye of a victim into an Evil Eye, transforming it into a precious stone the witch may see through as if under the effect of *Sight Beyond Sight. Hags* sometimes gift the Eyes as pendants, turning the wearer into an unwitting spy.

A trio of *Hags* is known as a coven and multiply their powers. If all three *Hags* are within the same geographic region (forest, mountains, plains, etc), they use Spells (6). If two *Hags* are in the region, Spells (4). A single *Hag* in a region may use Spells (2). Once per lunar cycle, a coven may perform a dark ritual to gain powers of prophecy.
