*Basilisks* are 14 ft lizards with eight legs, able to turn flesh to stone with their supernatural gaze. *Basilisk* lairs are often surrounded by broken, life like statues, which the monster consumes. Petrification may be reversed with the tears of a unicorn or sprite, or the *Flesh to Stone* spell.
