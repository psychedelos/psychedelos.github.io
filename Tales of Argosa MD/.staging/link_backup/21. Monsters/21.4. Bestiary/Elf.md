Elves have the qualities noted under the Race description. Culturally speaking, elves revere freedom, art, beauty, and the natural world, which sometimes brings them into conflict with expansionist humans and gold hungry dwarves.

Elves have an intrinsic attachment to nature, and can live for many human generations, fostering an aloofness and arrogance that encourages an isolationist view.
