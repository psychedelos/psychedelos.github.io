*Man Eating Monkeys* are 3 ft tall, agile primates with elongated teeth, extendable jaws and ferocious tempers. They are clever pack hunters, using hoots, howls, and sign language to communicate. While stalking their prey, *Man Eating Monkeys* take to the high branches to keep out of sight, dropping down to ambush their meal as they sleep. For *Man Eating Apes*, see the *Owlbear* entry.

**MAN EATING MONKEY** #5d6 **AC** 13 **HD** 1d4 hp **Bite** 1d6 **Nat 19** Disarmed **S**10 **D**17 **C**10 **I**4 **P**13 **W**8 **Ch**8 **L**3, **Leap** Close. When a target becomes *Wounded,* all *Monkeys* within Close range **frenzy,** ignoring other foes to attack the injured (they will risk free attacks to reposition). On a **Crit,** the *Monkey* climbs onto the back of the target and sinks its fangs into their neck, knocking them *Prone* (Str Contest to stand, no action). **Reac** 2-5 Hostile 6-8 Famished 9-10 Peaceful 11-12 Frolicsome.
