Exceedingly rare, and exceptionally dangerous, *Ogre Mages* are the result of *Ogre* shamans making dark pacts with entities beyond the Veil to become half demons. After the bargain is made, the shaman manifests horns, hooves, and a bloated, corpulent body, coupled with a keen intellect and unbridled cruelty. In exchange for their soul, the *Ogre* no longer ages, can shapeshift, and cast spells.
