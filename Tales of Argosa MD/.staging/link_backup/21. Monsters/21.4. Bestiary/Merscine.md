*Merscine* are 7 ft aquatic humanoids with shark or piranha like heads. They live in oceans, rivers, and lakes, but are quasi amphibious and can survive on land for a few hours if necessary. *Merscine* are strict carnivores of low intellect, highly attracted to shiny things which they horde in their underwater lairs. They communicate via clicks, whistles, and haunting tones reminiscent of whale songs.

**MERSCINE** #2d4 **AC** 12 **HD** 3 **Bite** 2d6 **Nat 19** A *Giant Shark* comes to the *Merscine's* aid (if possible) **S**16 **D**16 **C**12 **I**8 **P**10 **W**13 **Ch**10 **L**8, **Swim** Fast. A *Merscine* frenzies when a target becomes *Wounded*, attacking them if possible and ignoring Morale. Frenzied *Merscine* kill unconscious victims with their next action if the body is not recovered. **Reac** 2-6 Hostile 7-9 Starving 10-12 Unfriendly.
