| 1 | **Duck!** A stray crossbow bolt or distant marksmen takes a pot shot at the PC (attack roll at +4, 2d8+1 damage). |
| 2 | **Incoming!** Somebody hurls a brace of fire pots in the PC's vicinity; a *Luck* (Dex) save is required to avoid 3d6 damage and the *Burning* condition. |
| 3 | **Allied Reinforcements** appear from nearby trees, over a small rise, from another street, etc. If the PC is mid battle, enemies are automatically defeated (50% chance they are slaughtered, 50% they flee). |
| 4 | **Dead Weight** A hireling, henchmen, pet or important NPC accompanying the PC (if any) has been wounded and knocked unconscious until dusk/dawn. Can the PC keep them safe? |
| 5 | **Lost Weapon** The adventurer's weapon is somehow lost or destroyed (if magical it is damaged instead, but not usable until it is repaired, likely sometime after the battle). |
| 6 | **Backstab** A sneaky git stabs the PC in the back whilst they are preoccupied fighting others (1d8 + PC level damage). |
| 7 | **Lootin'** The PC seizes the opportunity to quickly loot nearby bodies. Consult the Bones to determine whether they gain *Carry Loot B* or *Valuables A.* |
| 8 | **Band of Brothers** Rallying cheers from the PC's surrounding allies reinvigorates the adventurer (restore up to half of lost hit points, a class ability use, or a *Reroll*). |
| 9 | **World of Hurt** A spear, javelin, etc strikes the PC in a vital location, causing *Missile Trauma* (1d10+2). |
| 10 | **Corpse Hazard** The PC threatens to become entangled in the growing pile of dead & dying underfoot: Dex (Acrobatics) check or fall prone, plus a further Str check to avoid dropping held items. |
| 11 | **Tide of Battle** Sheer numbers threaten to force the PC and their opponent into dangerous terrain (eg toppled off a wall, thrown into a raging river, knocked down a steep slope, pinned against a rock, pushed down a sewer chute, etc). A Str (Athletics) or Dex (Acrobatics) check resists. |
| 12 | **Crushing Intervention** A catapult stone, heavy warhorse, hurtling wagon, or other large moving object threatens to crush the PC. A *Luck* (Dex) save is required to avoid 2d6 damage and *Blunt Trauma* (1d6+6). |
| 13 | **Selfless Protector** If the PC is reduced to zero hp sometime during the mass battle, a nameless solider automatically intervenes to negate the attack. The soldier dies in the process. Once only, reroll repeats. |
| 14 | **Spoils of War** As the PC fells their latest foe, a valuable object is somehow revealed on the deceased (spills from a pouch, hangs about their neck, etc; 1 x *Valuables A*). |
| 15 | **Shaken Soul** A dreadful atrocity witnessed by the PC threatens to traumatize them. A *Luck* (Will) save is required to avoid a random Madness p.161. |
| 16 | **Fearsome Charge!** A small force of enemy cavalry or war beasts (pack of Hounds, Boars, Dire Wolves, etc), thunder through the area, leaving carnage in their wake! All PCs in Close range suffer *Blade Trauma* (1d10+2, a *Luck* (Con) save resists). Foes in the area are killed or scattered. |
| 17 | **Extreme Terrain** Terrain the PC is standing on or near becomes dangerous (a wall falls down, forest wildfire, bridge collapse, rockslide, choking smoke bomb, etc). The PC suffers 6d6 damage (an appropriate *Luck* save reduces damage by half). Nearby NPCs are similarly affected. |
| 18 | **Obscurement** A vision obscurement floods the area (heavy smoke from nearby fires, an alchemical smoke bomb, rolling fog, night time torches extinguished by freak winds, etc), limiting vision to Close range and imposing a 33% miss chance on attacks. Checks relating to hiding/escape gain adv. |
| 19 | **Snap Opportunity** A distant champion, leader, or other important enemy figure is momentarily exposed during the surging melee. Can the PCs take advantage of this fleeting opportunity to act against them? |
| 20 | **Sorcery!** An enemy mage or warrior with a one shot magical item unleashes it upon the PCs. Roll 1d4 (as 6th level): (i) *Brain Burst*, (ii) *Lightning Bolt*, (iii) *Blast of Frozen Ruin*, (iv) *Hellblast*. *Consult the Bones* to determine if there is a DDM effect. |
