Mass battle is resolved using the "**spotlight**" method, zooming in on what the party are doing across a series of **Field Events**. The events are dealt with "in media res", dropping the PCs into the action amidst the broader battle. The approach is intended to be abstract and improvised, obviating the need for miniatures or maps (of course these may be added if desired). The mass battle ends with the **Final Confrontation,** which determines the ultimate victor of the conflict.

### Field Events

The default is that PCs must face **six Field Events** before reaching the **Final Confrontation**, reflecting their part in the mass battle. Importantly, whatever situations arise, **the PCs are the only characters available** to deal with them. Their NPC allies, if any, are too busy managing other emergencies. The **usual procedure** is:

1. **PCs** decide their **starting positions** on the battlefield, based on the situation.
2. **Roll 1d20** on the chart to determine which **Field Events** arise (reroll repeats), choose entries according to the scenario, or prepare a sequence of events. Adding custom events is encouraged.
3. **The GM decides who is affected** by each event, based on the situation and who was involved in the last one; eg the **whole party**, **specific** or **random** PC(s), or the GM might allow the **players to choose.**
4. **The referee decides how** each Field Event is resolved on a **case by case** basis (fight, group check, montage, social, etc). **Simultaneous events** are possible, forcing the party to split.
5. **If some adventurers are not involved** in an event, but are in the vicinity of battle, *Consult the Bones* (GM's call if likely, unlikely, etc) to determine if one or more characters suffer a **Sudden Twist** p.164 while their allies are handling the latest event.
6. **After six Field Events** (or other number as the referee decides) the **Final Confrontation** occurs p.165 (which decides the victor).
