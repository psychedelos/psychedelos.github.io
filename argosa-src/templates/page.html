      How the Bones are read

      Yes and No cancel each other out. Exception: if the Judgment die is opposed by just one Twin of Fate, Judgment breaks the tie and that Fate die is ignored. A single result is an ordinary answer; a double is greater or more definitive; a triple is extreme or exaggerated.

      Sun on the Fortune die adds a benefit or advantage for the PCs; Skull adds a complication, setback or disadvantage; blank adds nothing.

      Likely / unlikely: if any die shows the answer you judged unlikely, reroll one of them, once. If the Judgment die shows it, the Judgment die must be the one rerolled.

      Dice: Judgment 3 Yes / 3 No. Each Twin 2 Yes / 2 No / 2 blank. Fortune 2 Sun / 2 Skull / 2 blank.
