Special Traits_#Boss\|Boss]]*). It has a limited vocab, but might spare the party for one of their mounts, or some valuable trinkets. |

| 14 | **Wyverns** The party has unwittingly entered the hunting grounds of 1d4 *[[Wyvern\|Wyverns]]*, whose lair is nearby. They are highly territorial, and will attempt to destroy or drive off intruders. |
| 15 | **Harpies** The haunting song of 2d6 *[[Harpy\|Harpies]]* can be heard on the wind before they descend from the clouds, appear from a mountain top, etc. They attempt to beguile at least one PC and lure them back to their lair (to be their plaything, and eventual meal). |

| 18 | **Bats** A flock of bats flies through or over the party, chasing insects. A random character is accidentally hit by a bat or their droppings; *Luck* (Con) save to avoid a random disease. |
| 19 | **Griffons** A flock of 2d6 *[[Griffon\|Griffons]]* fly around the party's ship, playing some kind of game, or attack them if they are riding flying mounts (or using personal flying magic). |
| 20 | **Wasps** 2d4 *[[Wasp, Giant\|Giant Wasps]]* are conveying 1d6 paralysed humans to their nest. One captive has regained movement in her face, giving the party a desperate look as they buzz by. |
