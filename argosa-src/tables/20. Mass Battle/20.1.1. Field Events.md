| 3 | **Death From Above!** Siege weapons hammer the area the PCs are in. Roll 1d6: (i) boulders, (ii) fire, (iii) toxic gas, (iv) ballista bolts, (v) diseased heads, (vi) Nets. Use [[26.1. Traps_\|Traps]] to improvise effects. |
| 4 | **Fire & Flame!** A large blaze has broken out nearby (building, wagon, corpse pile, trees, grasslands, etc), thick smoke filling the air. If the PCs don't take action, the fire will swiftly spread/grow into a raging inferno! |
| 5 | **Innocents In Peril** One or more non-combatants are in danger (eg 2d6 civilians being dragged away by foes, 1d4 stowaway children cowering beneath a supply wagon about to be attacked, an allied warrior's helm is knocked off revealing a highborn's underage heir). Will the PCs intervene? |
| 6 | **Hold the Line!** 3d6 enemies are about to break through the shield wall or other defensive line close to the PCs. Can they hold the line? |
| 7 | **A Friend in Need** Through the carnage, the PCs spot a wounded NPC friend slowly but inevitably being encircled by 2d6 enemies as his/her allies are cut down. Can the PCs save their mate? |
| 8 | **And My Axe** During a furious melee, one PC spots an exhausted soldier drop to both knees after felling multiple foes. Behind him, another enemy readies her spear to skewer him. Will the PC intervene? If saved, the grateful soldier pledges to repay his rescuer (a free random henchmen for one year). |
| 9 | **Frantic Beasts** Nearby animals (roll 1d4: (i) war dogs, (ii) mules, (iii) chargers, (iv) supply horses with carts) have been spooked by the death of their handlers, siege weapons, fire, foul weather, etc. Can the PCs help calm the beasts, or will they break free and wreak havoc? |
| 10 | **With Me Brothers!** Soldiers fighting alongside the PCs are losing heart due to recent setbacks (roll 1d4: (i) captain killed, (ii) suffered heavy losses, (iii) serious fatigue, (iv) enemy magic) and are on the verge of breaking. Can the PCs rally them? |
| 11 | **Traitorous Scum** 2d6 traitors have been hired or coerced to kill the PCs mid battle by an old nemesis, enemy forces, unknown threat, etc. The traitors are each armed with one dose of [[26.4.18. Poison\|moderate poison]]. |
| 12 | **In the Thick of It** The PCs are battling where the fighting is fiercest, hewing enemies left and right in a desperate bid to survive. But as one foe falls, another takes its place. How much longer can the PCs endure? Treat as a combat with 3d6 foes. At the end all PCs must make a *Luck* (Con) save or be *[[12.11. Conditions_#Fatigued\|Fatigued]]*. |
| 13 | **Behind Enemy Lines** A fleeting chance to gain a battlefield advantage arises if the party can sneak behind enemy lines. Roll 1d4 for their mission: (i) sabotage enemy supplies, (ii) assassinate a 6 HD leader, (iii) gather crucial intelligence about war plans, troop movements, reinforcements, etc, (iv) steal or neutralise a secret weapon. Can the PCs infiltrate enemy territory, get the job done, and make it back in one piece? |
| 14 | **Right Place Right Time** As the PC drops another foe, they spot disaster about to unfold a short distance away! Roll 1d3: (i) battering ram or siege tower flounders when one of the handlers is killed, (ii) dying standard bearer can't raise her signal banner at a crucial time, (iii) a riderless messenger horse is rearing in panic, undelivered orders visible in the saddle bag. Can the PCs complete the task in time? |
| 15 | **Fall Back!** As the battle unfolds, PCs and nearby soldiers are forced to withdraw, driven back by overwhelming odds. Can the adventurers escape? |
| 16 | **Sniper Hunt** A hidden sharpshooter (siege tower, tree line, valley ridge, high building, etc) is targeting the PCs' area, protecting enemy champions and picking off key allied warriors (sergeants, standard bearers, medics, etc). Can the PCs find the sniper and neutralise them? |
| 17 | **Medic!** Through the smoky haze, urgent cries for a medic ring out as a dying NPC is dragged to relative safety (roll 1d3: (i) messenger with critical orders, (ii) scout with intel, (iii) highly respected sergeant good for morale). The ally is on the verge of death; can the PCs save him/her? |
| 18 | **Shields Up!** A barrage of arrows, spears, bolts, fire pots, and other personal ranged weapons target the PCs' location. Soldiers without shields or that can't find cover drop like flies. PCs require a *Luck* (Dex) save or 3d6 damage and *[[13.2.4. Missile Trauma\|Missile Trauma]]* (1d10+2). |
| 19 | **Dark Ritual** Word somehow reaches the PCs in the midst of combat that an enemy warlock, sorceress, priest, etc (5+ HD, *[[23.2. Consult the Bones_\|Consult the Bones]]* to determine if a *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Heavy\|Heavy]]* or *[[21.3. Special Traits_#Boss\|Boss]]*) is performing a dark ritual elsewhere on the battlefield (gathering a bowl of hearts to offer to the dark gods, etc). Can the PCs stop the ritual? |
| 20 | **Suicide Mission** A genuine suicide mission presents itself (eg: hold the tide of enemies on the bridge so allies can escape, light the warning beacon atop the tower surrounded by foes, collapse the tunnel taking out a tide of foes as well as oneself). Will the PC(s) sacrifice themselves for the greater good? If so, the PC must make a Death save. If successful, roll on the *[[13.1.1. Injuries & Setbacks\|Injuries & Setbacks]]* table, and they awaken later with 1 hp, somehow left for dead. If none of the PCs take the mission, add a complication to the Final Confrontation. |
